A CV for this job, not every job

A generic CV loses to a specific one. Most people know that and still send the same CV everywhere, because rewriting it for every application takes an evening.

freehire does that rewrite with you. Start from a job: run the match analysis, then tailor from its result. It copies your CV for that one vacancy — your original is never touched — and pulls forward the experience you already have but buried three roles down.

It does not invent anything.

When the job asks for something your history doesn’t support, it asks you instead of writing it in. Every change is one edit you can see and undo, and the result is a clean PDF an ATS can actually read.
