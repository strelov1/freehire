You don’t have to interview alone

If you’ve applied anywhere lately, chances are someone on freehire applied to the same companies — and one of them may have just had the interview you’re preparing for.

Interview buddies lets you find them. Join under a random handle and you’ll see members looking for the same kind of role, in the same place, and how far they got: applied, interview, offer.

Then ask the one who’s been there.

Send a short first message — how the loop went, what they asked, how long the silence lasted. They can reply or decline, and declining is silent.

Nobody sees your name, your email or your CV — only your handle, your role titles and your progress. Rejections never show up in the feed. It’s free.
