A side panel on any job page

One more thing worth knowing about: freehire has a browser extension, and it works on job pages that have nothing to do with us.

Open the side panel on a posting — Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, or a career page nobody has heard of. It reads the page itself, scores the role against your CV, and fills the application form out of your profile. You read what it wrote and press Submit.

The point is that it works where you already are.

It signs in with the same account, so it is your CV and your profile it fills from — nothing to set up a second time.
