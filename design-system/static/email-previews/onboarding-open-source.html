Why this thing is open

Some background you might not know: freehire is fully open source. Every parser, every dedup rule, the ranking — all of it is public. Nothing is hidden about how a job gets in, or why it sits where it sits.

I built it in the open because job search runs on trust, and “trust me” is a poor answer from a site that decides what you get to see.

If it has been useful, a star is how most people end up finding the project:
