**Hubstaff Talent traps** (`hubstafftalent`; live-verified 2026-11-18 from a residential IP, not yet from the crawl host):

- **No API, no job sitemap.** `talent.hubstaff.com` 302s every path onto `hubstafftalent.net`, the host the adapter uses. `/sitemap.xml` is a 2021 Screaming Frog dump of country and category pages; `/search/jobs.json` is a 500. The job pages carry no JSON-LD.
- **The listing is a Rails UJS answer.** `/search/jobs?page=N` is an empty shell for a plain GET (`#results` is filled by script). With `X-Requested-With: XMLHttpRequest` and `Accept: text/javascript` it answers `$('#results').html("...")`, the markup as a string literal escaped by Rails' `escape_javascript`: JSON's escapes plus `\$`, `\'` and `` \` ``, which a JSON decoder rejects (`railsJSString`). 15 postings a page, ~48 pages (714 on 2026-11-18), newest first. Past the end the page holds no `.search-result` card. The `Accept` must reach the wire: the shared client used to overwrite a caller's `Accept` with `text/html`, which gets the empty shell.
- **Cloudflare rate-limits hard (429, error 1015, `Retry-After` ~53s).** Eight parallel pages or one a second got 4 of 10 refused; one request every 6s was answered 25 of 25. The adapter paces every request, listing and detail, at one per 6s (one per 5s was refused at page 15 of a walk): a listing walk is ~5 minutes, and at most 120 new postings are hydrated per run (~12 minutes), newest first, the rest on later runs. The first full backfill of ~700 postings therefore takes several runs.
- **The card has no body**, only a clipped bio, so a new posting costs one page request; a stored one is a free liveness refresh (`SeenRefresh`). The body is `div.job-description` on the posting page.
- **Pay is honest or absent.** `.pay-rate` holds `$N/hr` for hourly postings (mapped to an hourly USD salary). `.fixed-price` holds a project budget, which is not a rate and is not read. Many postings show "Negotiable rate". Label `full time` is full-time; `hourly` and `fixed price` are `contract`.
- **Age tooltip is UTC**: `data-original-title="November 18, 2026 at  9:45 pm"` (the card's "4 hrs ago" text agrees with it).
- **Slug is the id**, `/jobs/<slug>` (`customer-success-manager-36d8fc`); the client is `a.job-agency` and its href is the client's own website. Cards without a client are dropped.
- **Postings are remote** (every card carries the "Remote job" icon); the location shown is the client's HQ, not where the work may be done, so it is kept as location text only.
