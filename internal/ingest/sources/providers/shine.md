**Shine traps** (`shine`; live-verified 2026-10-25 from a residential IP and the crawl host):

- **Plain HTTP under our own User-Agent passes**, from the crawl host as from anywhere: the sitemaps and the posting pages answer 200 in ~1 s. `robots.txt` disallows `/api/*` (so no JSON search), `/job-search/` variants and two id prefixes under `/jobs/1*` / `/jobs/9*` that are not posting URLs; the posting pages (`/jobs/<title>/<company>/<id>`) are allowed, and the only named user agents are `Scrapy` and `Trabajo`.
- **The sitemaps are the listing.** `index-all-sitemap.xml` names `all_jd_sitemap.xml` and `all_jd_<n>_sitemap.xml` (10,000 URLs each): 222,925 distinct postings on 2026-10-25, ids 19.4M–19.93M bar a few dozen older ones, `lastmod` spread over the last week (~10k a day). The index is regenerated daily and names only the files that exist — `all_jd_23_sitemap.xml` answered 500 the same afternoon it had answered 200, after the index stopped naming it. Any NAMED file failing fails the crawl. Not `fullCatalog`: nothing states a total the walk could be checked against.
- **Both slugs are cosmetic; the id is the identity.** `/jobs/x/y/19513074` serves the posting; a gone id answers 404, read as gone. The URL spells the title and the employer (`/jobs/senior-data-engineer-1/mastercard/19679658`), which is what makes the adapter `CoverageGated` without a request: the gate is asked about the employer the URL spells and judges the title it spells, and a condemned posting is yielded body-less.
- **The page's schema.org JobPosting is the posting.** `description` is the body (plain text with line breaks); `datePosted` is RFC 3339 in IST; `experienceRequirements.monthsOfExperience` is the floor of the range the page shows ("4 to 8 Yrs" is 48) and becomes `ExperienceYearsMin`; `baseSalary` (6 of 40 sampled) is INR a YEAR in rupees, mapped as stated; `jobLocation[]` carries locality and region per place ("All India" names no city and is rewritten to `India`). `occupationalCategory` is NOT a signal — 28 of 40 sampled postings, hotels and banks among them, read "General / Other Software" — so no tech hint is set; ~7 of 40 sampled titles were technical. `addressCountry` is always `IN`, including on reposted US adverts, so it only feeds `FallbackCountries`.
- **Many posters are consultancies or individual recruiters** ("Headstart Manpower Consultants.", "MD SHAKEEL AHAMED"), and some repost adverts from other aggregators. They are filed under the name the posting gives; the cross-source duplicate suppression is what handles a copy.
- **The run is bounded.** One limiter paces every request at one a second with two workers, and a run reads at most `shineMaxNewPerRun` (1,500, 25 minutes) unseen postings, newest id first; at the default two-hour cadence that is ~18k a day against ~10k new, so the cold backlog drains in about four weeks, less whatever the gate condemns. The bound is NOT the whole run: the ~22 sitemaps (~2 minutes), the coverage probe over ~62k employers and the writes after the fetch (~7 new a second from the crawl host) share the same 2,400 s crawl budget, and a hydrating run that meets its deadline stores NOTHING, since every write follows the fetch. The first runs (2026-10-25..27) all died that way: the coverage probe resolved company aliases one employer at a time, ~62k round trips at ~36 ms, ~37 minutes, and each run read fewer than a hundred pages before its deadline (fixed in the pipeline: the probe now resolves the board in one batch).
