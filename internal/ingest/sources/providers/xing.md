**XING traps** (`xing`; live-verified 2026-10-25 from the crawl host, over 400 randomly sampled postings):

- **The sitemaps are the listing, and they are gzip FILES.** robots.txt disallows `/jobs/search` and `/graphql/`; `/jobs/sitemap.xml.gz` names eleven `<n>-sitemap.xml.gz` files of 50,000 URLs each — 519,661 distinct postings on 2026-10-24. They are served as `application/gzip` with no `Content-Encoding`, so the transport does not inflate them: the adapter reads them through `GetStream` and gunzips itself. The index also lists role/skill/city landing sitemaps, which are not postings. Plain HTTP passes from host2 under the project's own User-Agent; no Scrapling, no proxy.
- **Not `fullCatalog`.** Nothing states a total to check the walk against, and the sitemap lags: 3.4% of sampled URLs already answered **410 Gone**, whose page caches an `UnauthorizedJob` instead of a `VisibleJob`. Such a posting is not yielded; a stored one stays open until the sitemap drops it.
- **The posting is read from the page's Apollo cache**, the `window.crate=` assignment in `<script id="runtime-config">`. It is a JavaScript object literal, not JSON — `undefined` appears in value positions and is rewritten to `null`. The ld+json is NOT read: its `description` for a TemplateData posting is the sections concatenated with a literal `null` and bare headings, and its `baseSalary.unitText` is `YEAR` on every salary, hourly ones included. The cache has everything the ld+json has plus the fields below.
- **Two body shapes.** `HtmlDescription.content` (80%), or `TemplateData` (20%): a `genericDescription` and/or sections each with its own heading, kept as `<h3>`. The `companyDescription` section is the employer's standing blurb and is left out.
- **`remoteOptions`: FULL_REMOTE → remote, PARTLY_REMOTE → hybrid, NON_REMOTE → nothing.** NON_REMOTE is the form's default, not a statement: 17 of 321 NON_REMOTE postings offered home office in their own text, one titled "100% Remote deutschlandweit". Shares in the sample: NON_REMOTE 83%, PARTLY_REMOTE 17%, FULL_REMOTE 0.6%.
- **`salary.__typename` says whose figure it is.** `SalaryEstimate` (72%) is XING's guess for the role and is never stored; `SalaryRange`/`Salary` (8.5%) is the employer's ("Unternehmensangabe" on the page). XING states no period for it — "14 € – 18 €", "2.800 € – 3.400 €" and "65.000 € – 80.000 €" sit in the same field — so only a figure of at least 20,000 is stored, as yearly; nothing in DACH pays that per hour, day or month, and no full-time year pays less.
- **Country comes from `locations[].country.countryCode`** (DE 91%, CH 6%, AT 3% of places). It is not always right — a Belgian city came back `DE` — but it is right on every DACH place sampled, and the location string `City, Region` ("Werthenstein, Kanton Luzern") is what the dictionary reads for the city. A posting may name up to ten places.
- **`discipline` 1011 ("IT und Softwareentwicklung", 7.7%) sets `IsTechHint`**: its titles were developers, admins, SAP consultants, IT apprenticeships. The suffix after the dot in a cache id (`Discipline:1011.6cf3f7`) is a query hash, not part of the value.
- **~42% of postings are applied to on XING itself** (`JobXingApplication`/`JobXingCustomApplication`); the rest carry a `UrlApplication` to an ATS or a job ad network. They are NOT filtered: the sitemap names no employer, so nothing is known before the page is bought, and the pipeline's aggregator-coverage gate already discards a posting whose employer we crawl from its own board — measured, 105 of 387 sampled postings (27%; arbeitsagentur 47, join 33, personio 8, successfactors 8). The rest, ATS-applied ones included, come from boards we do not crawl.
- **That gate is why the run has two lanes.** A discarded posting is never stored, so it stays unseen and is bought again every run. Newest-first alone would stall: once the discarded postings above the backlog outnumbered the budget (~8,000 hydrations in), no older posting would ever be reached. So 1,200 of a run's 2,000 detail requests take the newest unseen ids (~7,000 new ids reach the sitemap a day) and 800 take a window of the rest that moves every hour. One limiter paces every request at 1/s; a page is ~360 KB and answers in ~0.5 s.
- **Not an IT board.** Production, healthcare, engineering and sales dominate; recruiters post one job per town (one agency listed the same tax role under fifteen consecutive ids, one village each).
