**Velora HR traps** (`velorahr`, a Spanish ATS at `<tenant>.career.velorahr.com/<slug>-<site id>`; live-verified 2026-10-27 — 63 career sites among ids 1..345, 1,146 distinct offers, mostly cleaning, catering, retail and hospitality: Serunion/Derichebourg 312, Grupo Batta (Mexico) 300, Vítaly 94):
- **The board is the numeric CAREER-SITE id, not the tenant host**: one tenant runs several sites (Serunion's host serves Serunion, Derichebourg, Singularis, an internship site and a master). The public API is keyed by it — `GET api.velorahr.com/api/public/career-site/<id>` (the site; 404 when there is none) and `…/<id>/offers?filter={}&range=[0,999]&sort=["id","DESC"]`, which returns every offer WITH its description and requirements, so nothing is hydrated and a crawl is two requests per board. Without `range` the listing stops at 21. An unknown id answers the offers call with an empty 200, which is why the site is read first: its 404 is `ErrBoardGone`. A listing that fills the 1,000-offer window fails the crawl (the adapter is `fullBoardListing`; the largest site is 310). The site page renders whatever slug precedes the id (`/158` and `/wetaca-158` are one page), so `atsboard` recognises `<tenant>.career.velorahr.com/<anything>-<id>/…` as board `<id>`.
- **A MASTER site (`"master": true` in its configuration; Serunion's 279) re-lists its child sites' offers, each carrying `career_site_id` = its owner** — an own offer carries null. The adapter skips the foreign ones, so an offer is stored once, under its owner's board.
- **Twin sites list the SAME offers** (an internal and an external site of one tenant: Circet 149/199, Magic Costa Blanca 80/147, OCN 272/285, Voltereta 267/268, …), and since the pipeline namespaces a posting by board, crawling both stores each offer twice. `harvest-boards velorahr` (no seed: it walks the ids itself until 100 in a row have no site) proposes a site only when it owns an offer no other site owns, keeping the lower id of two equal sets. A twin whose set later grows apart from its sibling is missed until the next harvest.
- **The vendor's demo sites are dressed as prospects** (one calls itself Inditex, another Kids&Us); they live on tenant hosts named `demo-…`/`…-demo-…`, which the prober declines by the configuration's `public_url`. `Promoción Interna` (205) is Serunion's internal-promotion site and `Programa de prácticas` (206) its internship site; both name the site, not the employer, in `company_name`, so they were left out of the catalogue by hand.
- **Mapping**: `clean_title` is the title without the `[WRDTDPC]` reference code; `country` is ISO (ES, MX, PT, MT seen), `work_mode` is `presencial`/`hibrido`/`remoto`, `work_schedule` `Jornada completa`/`Media jornada`/`Por horas`/`Indiferente`. A salary is published only under `show_salary` (`salary` reads `hidden` otherwise, while `salary_from`/`salary_to` still hold placeholders like 1000), with its period in `optional_fields.salaryType` — and `optional_fields` may be null. A bilingual town (`Pamplona/Iruña`, `Alicante/Alacant`) keeps its Spanish half, and the location line ends in the COUNTRY CODE: measured over the 456 distinct places, "town, province, ES" resolves the town itself for 331 against 219 for "town, province", where the dictionary reads the province as a second city.
