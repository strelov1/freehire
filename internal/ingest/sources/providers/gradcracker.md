**Gradcracker traps** (`gradcracker`; live-verified 2026-10-18 from the crawl host, fsn and a residential IP):

- **Cloudflare twice over: a fingerprint gate, then a per-IP RATE limit.** curl and Go's default TLS get a managed challenge (403 "Just a moment...") on every path but `robots.txt`; `fingerprintHTTP` (Chrome_144) is served 200 keyless. Then, at one request per 3 s (or 0.5 s, cookie jar or not) the edge served ~35 requests and refused every path from that IP for **55–65 minutes**, identically on all three IPs; at one per 10 s it served 60 posting pages from one IP and the whole 37-page listing walk from another without a refusal. `gradcrackerRequestInterval` is that 10 s, through one limiter for every request of a run; the first refusal ends the run's hydration, since the IP is blocked for the next hour anyway.
- **One boardless catalogue, not a board per discipline.** A posting is listed under every discipline it accepts — 1,018 of electronic-electrical's 1,350 were also among computing-technology's 1,455 — and `external_id` is namespaced by board, so per-discipline boards would store them twice. The adapter walks both disciplines (`gradcrackerDisciplines`) and keeps each posting once: 1,787 postings in all on 2026-10-18.
- **`fullCatalog`, proven by the listing's own count.** `?order=dateAdded` pages carry 80 cards, a page past the last answers 404, and the raw card count of a full walk equals the listing's `Show N results` exactly. Any page failing, or a walk short of that count by more than a few cards, fails the run instead of letting the source-scoped sweep close what it missed.
- **The listing carries a honeypot card**: a visually hidden `<article wire:key="hp">` linking a "featured graduate employer" (`/hub/9000001/.../graduate-job/1/...`). Only an article whose `wire:key` is the posting id in its link is a posting.
- **No JSON-LD anywhere.** The card is everything but the body (title, employer as the logo's `alt`, Location/Salary/Deadline/Degree/Duration/Starting, accepted disciplines); the body is the posting page's `.body-content`, which embeds video/insight panels (`not-body-content`), a feedback-pledge badge and a content hash that are stripped. The card's facts are prepended to the body as a list, since the start date, duration and degree appear nowhere else. The sitemap (one 2 MB request) names every live posting but no discipline, so it cannot discover anything and the adapter does not read it.
- **Grades come from the title dictionary.** The opportunity type in the URL sets only `EmploymentType=internship` for `work-placement-internship`; seniority is `internal/dict/classify`'s, which learned the UK vocabulary for this source ("Graduate" → junior, "Industrial Placement"/"Year in Industry"/"Summer Placement"/"Vacation Scheme" → intern). The rest of the unresolved titles are mostly not jobs at all (webinars, insight evenings, open days) and the catalogue filter decides those.
