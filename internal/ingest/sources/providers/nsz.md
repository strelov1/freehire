**NSZ traps** (`nsz`, the job search of Serbia's National Employment Service, nsz.gov.rs; live-verified 2026-10-29 from a residential IP):

- **A small catalogue beside a large one.** 510 open vacancies from 362 employers on 2026-10-29, 20-60 published each weekday, mostly health care, education, the army and local employers; the site's "Informatika i programiranje" field held 2. `infostud` held 2,946 open Serbian postings the same day and only 3 of NSZ's employers by exact name; no stored posting links nsz.gov.rs. EURES has no Serbian board.
- **The "LAT" switch is not a request.** It calls `setLang('latinica')`, which stores the choice in `localStorage` and runs the jQuery transliterate plugin over the page in the browser; `?lang=` changes only the interface language (`lat` and `en` give the English interface), never the posted text. The server always sends what the employer typed: 457 of 510 titles were Cyrillic, the rest Latin. So the adapter transliterates every kept field itself, with `normalize.LatinizeSerbian` (Gaj's alphabet, one-to-one, the mapping `classify` reads BCS titles with); Latin text passes through unchanged.
- **The search is the server-rendered page `/employee/jobs/search?page=N`**, 20 cards a page, newest id first, ~3-6 s a page; the pager links the last page (26). A page past the last answers 200 with no card. The walk ends on an empty page past the pager's last and fails on any page failing, on an empty page short of it, on a page without the search form, on an empty catalogue, and at 200 pages. A walk measured 510 distinct ids for 510 listed. robots.txt allows everything; the default User-Agent is served, no cookie needed.
- **A card is "employer town"**, the two separated by a run of spaces (`nszSplitEmployer` cuts at the last run); the town is the employer's, not the place of work. Employers are register names, sometimes with their address ("MINISTARSTVO ODBRANE VP 3947 21131 NOVI SAD, AVIJATIČARSKA 24"), and are kept as written.
- **The body is the vacancy page `/employee/jobs/preview/<id>`** (~50 KB, ~1.4 s), read for new vacancies only, newest first, 1,200 a run at one request a second, stopping 5 minutes before the deadline. An id the site no longer shows 302s to the home page: that is "gone"; a page without the vacancy block is an error. The body is `div.job-content` without its closing gender notice ("НАПОМЕНА: Сви изрази, појмови…", on every vacancy), then the "Подаци о послу" table's facts as a list. The table's `Напомена` (note) is where the employer writes a contact person, a phone and an e-mail, and is never copied. The period ("29.10.2026. - 28.11.2026.") gives the posting date and the application deadline (its closing day, read into `ApplicationDeadline`); `Место рада` the place of work, several separated by semicolons; the occupational field (the first span of `job-location`) "Информатика и програмирање" sets `IsTechHint`. There is no salary and no remote flag.
- **`Врста рада`**: "Уговор о делу", "Привремени и повремени послови" and "Рад на пројекту" are contract work (3 of 510). "Неодређено" (permanent, 162) and "Одређено" (fixed term, 216) say nothing about working time and are not mapped, nor is "Волонтирање".
- **The country is the source's (`rs`)**: the places are Serbian municipalities (one multi-place vacancy listed Priština among eight Serbian cities). Measured over all 510: the title dictionary resolved a category for 436 (85%), the location dictionary a country for 463 (91%) — the misses are small municipalities (Čajetina, Irig, Paraćin), which the structural country covers.
