**HelloWork traps** (`hellowork`; live-verified 2026-11-22 from the prod crawl host, host2):

- **robots.txt disallows `/*?`, so every query string is off limits.** That removes the search page (`/fr-fr/emploi/recherche.html` is also named outright) and the pager of every listing: the page numbers are a `<form id="paginationForm">` that submits `p` as a query parameter. `/fr-fr/offres/postuler`, `/fr-fr/offres/smart`, `/fr-fr/api/`, `/fr-fr/emplois/ext/` and `/fr-fr/emplois/token/` are disallowed too; `/fr-fr/emplois/<id>.html` and the `/fr-fr/emploi/...` landing pages are not. The adapter never builds or follows a URL with `?`, and a test holds that.
- **The sitemap is unreachable.** robots.txt names one, `/fr-fr/horizon.xml` (`/sitemap.xml` redirects to it), and Cloudflare answers it 403 "Sorry, you have been blocked" to plain curl, to the Scrapling `http` tier with and without the proxy (4 of 4 refused) and to its `browser` mode. Nothing that answers the other pages answers that file, so there is no sitemap route.
- **What is left is a window onto the catalogue.** The clean-URL landing pages `/fr-fr/emploi/domaine_<field>.html` (~60, all linked from the home page), `metier_<job>.html` (a field page links 32, a job page 44 more), `domaine_<field>-ville_<city>.html` and `mot-cle_<word>.html` each show the 20 leading postings and link dozens of siblings. Measured over 60 pages on 2026-11-22: 19.5 new ids a page, 1,168 distinct ids, and the link graph does not run out. The site's counters put the catalogue in the hundreds of thousands (the Informatique page alone says "25 550 offres"), so a run reads a window and never the whole: HelloWork's own pager is the only way past the first 20 of a page.
- **The window moves.** The home page's links to landing pages are read first. The remaining page budget sets the width of the sorted frontier slice and the distance its offset advances per hour (`helloworkRotate`). Links resolve against the final page URL and must lead to a clean HelloWork posting or landing path. The order of postings on a landing page has not been verified.
- **Expiry and liveness.** The adapter stores the calendar date in `validThrough` as the inclusive application deadline. The sampled postings expire 30 days after `datePosted`, which is also the unseen sweep's grace period. The liveness worker checks up to 500 older unseen postings per run, at one clean, ID-matching HelloWork posting URL per second. It closes a posting after two 404/410 responses and stops the pass on refusal. A stored posting that reappears in the listings is refreshed body-less (`SeenRefresh`).
- **Access: plain HTTP, no proxy, no browser.** The project's own User-Agent (`freehire/0.1 (+https://freehire.me)`) and the browser-compatible form were both served 200 on a landing page, a posting page and the home page from host2 (3 of 3 each); the Scrapling http+proxy route also answered 200 on the pages. The adapter paces requests at one a second and handles both retries (403/429/5xx, 2 s backoff doubling each time). A 404/410 is the site's "gone" (`/fr-fr/emplois/70000000.html` answers 410, `/fr-fr/emplois/1.html` 404). A detail failure yields an unreadable marker carrying the posting's id and URL when other attempted details answered. A posting page is ~230 KB.
- **The body is the ld+json JobPosting** on `/fr-fr/emplois/<id>.html` (the `type` attribute is entity-encoded, `application/ld&#x2B;json`, which the HTML parser decodes; the page also carries a BreadcrumbList block and a WebSite one, and `ldJobPosting` picks the JobPosting). `title`, `description` (HTML, a few KB), `datePosted` (`2026-11-22T09:36:25Z`), `hiringOrganization.name`, `jobLocation.address` (`addressLocality`, `addressRegion` as the region name; older postings may carry only a region, `Champagne-Ardenne`), `occupationalCategory` (the field the posting is filed under: IT, commerce, food service, construction), `skills`, `industry`, `validThrough`. The external id is the number in the URL.
- **`employmentType` is a string or a list** (`FULL_TIME`, `["TEMPORARY","FULL_TIME"]`, `["CONTRACTOR","FULL_TIME"]`, `["TEMPORARY","PART_TIME"]`). The kind of contract wins over the hours (a fixed-term full-time posting is `contract`).
- **`jobLocationType: TELECOMMUTE` is not read.** It sits on a CGI Grenoble posting whose body says "jusqu'a 3 jours de teletravail par semaine" and on other Informatique postings, so it means remote work is possible, not that the posting is remote. Mapping it would tag hybrid roles as remote. Work mode is left to the dictionaries.
- **Salary is read from the numeric bounds only.** `baseSalary.value` carries `minValue`/`maxValue` with `unitText` YEAR or MONTH (sampled: 35,000-42,000 YEAR; 1,865 MONTH, no max), sometimes only a formatted string (`"value":"38 000,00"`, ignored) and often an empty QuantitativeValue. `estimatedSalary` is HelloWork's own estimate, not the employer's statement, and is never read.
- **`IsTechHint` follows the source's own filing:** `occupationalCategory` of `Informatique` or `Data et IA` (the labels of the `domaine_informatique` and `domaine_data-et-ia` fields). `Data et IA` was not sampled, only `Informatique`; confirm the label on the first run's rows.
- **The catalogue is French, so titles resolve a category worse than an English board's.** Expect a materially larger share of postings with no category from `internal/dict/classify` (French titles such as "Developpeur", "Chef de projet", "Serveur", "Aide-soignant"); measure it on the first run before touching the dictionary.
- **The run is bounded.** 300 landing pages plus at most 1,200 unseen postings at one request a second take about 25 minutes before retries. The detail budget reserves 900 requests for the newest IDs and 300 for a rotating window of older unseen IDs. A crawl fails if every landing page failed or every requested posting was unreadable.
