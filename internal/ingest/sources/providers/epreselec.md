**ePreselec traps** (`epreselec`, Adevinta's ATS — the InfoJobs owner's — at `<label>.epreselec.com`, used by large Spanish employers: FCC, Ineco, Ilunion, Paradores, Applus+, Compass Group; live-verified 2026-10-27 — 53 labels from Common Crawl and a web search, ~33 with openings, ~2,100 offers, the largest `grupoexterna` 295, `eeseleccion` 291, `fcc` 259, `meditempus` 259, `ineco` 203):

- **Imperva fronts every site.** The crawl host, a laptop and the fixed proxy exit all draw "Pardon Our Interruption" (HTTP 405 through a proxy, 200 to a browser UA direct) after a handful of requests, and the Scrapling browser over the proxy is refused too. The impersonating fetcher over the ROTATING exits passes about half the requests, so the provider is a `scraplingProviders` row (http + proxy) and the adapter re-sends a 405 up to eight times (four lost 41% of the first crawl's bodies). Consequence: `harvest-boards` cannot probe it from either host — add boards with `add-board` after probing through the tier.
- **One unpaginated list, `/Ofertas/Ofertas.aspx`, states its count** ("Total ofertas: N"), and the walk must read exactly N anchors carrying `data_idOferta` — the adapter is `fullBoardListing`. The anchors' href is an ASP.NET postback; the detail is the same page with `?id_oferta=<id>`, which fills `.onepage-oferta` (h3 Funciones/Requisitos/Se ofrece, each followed by a `<p>`).
- **A detail page carries the whole list again** (50-700 KB a body), so the adapter hydrates new offers only, at most 120 per board per run.
- **An unknown or closed label answers 200 with the platform's own "404 - Page not found" page** (DNS is a wildcard), which carries no list panel and is a board-level error.
- **`op-perfil` is the employer BRAND**, not the site: FCC's site lists Aqualia, FCC Infraestructuras and FCC Servicios Medioambientales.
