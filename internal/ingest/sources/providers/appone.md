**AppOne traps** (`appone`, Paychex; live-verified 2026-10-20 from a residential IP):

- **The board is the job-portal slug, and a posting URL does not carry it.** `jobs.appone.com/<slug>` is the portal; `GET jobs.appone.com/api/portal/v1/companyjobposts/<slug>` lists it (title, location, job type, `workplaceType`, a structured `salary`, `datePosted`) and names the employer. A posting is `apply.appone.com/job/<24-hex id>`, whose record `GET apply.appone.com/api/apply/v2/jobposting/<id>` carries the plain-text `description` and the portal as `jobPortalUrl` — which is the ONLY place the portal is named. So `atsboard` recognises the portal URL and declines the posting URL, and `cmd/harvest-boards appone` resolves a posting-URL seed with that one request (`seedResolver`). 2 of 40 sampled postings had an empty `jobPortalUrl` and belong to no board at all. Slugs are case-insensitive; the stored board is the platform's own spelling.
- **The listing stops at 100 postings** and ignores every paging parameter tried (`page`, `pageNumber`, `pageSize`, `skip`, `offset`); one sampled portal held exactly 100. That is why `appone` does not claim `fullBoardListing`.
- **The edge answers 403, never 429, when it judges a client too fast** — a bare `Forbidden` from Azure Front Door, on both hosts. ~100 requests back to back turned into 38 refusals in 40; at one request a second refusals still came in bursts a few minutes in, and at one every two seconds too (0-14 per 20 over seven minutes), so no measured pace removes them. The run is paced at 1 req/s across all boards (`pacedAppOneGetter`), the adapter retries a 403 twice with a doubling pause, and a detail still refused is an `Unreadable` marker that the next run asks for again. Measured: a cold run over six portals (one of them at the 100 cap) left 0 of 100, 5 of 51, 5 of 12, 3 of 36, 6 of 18 and 1 of 2 details unreadable; a later run over six small portals, 2 of 42.
- **The legacy AppOne product is not this one and is not crawled.** `www.appone.com/MainInfoReq.asp?R_ID=…&SearchScreenID=…` (also served as `recruiting.myapps.paychex.com/appone/…`) is a classic ASP site: its search page is an ASP.NET postback keyed by a `ServerVar` (`<company>.appone.com`) that the posting URL does not carry, nothing public lists by `SearchScreenID`, and the "other openings" block on a posting page is capped at five.
