**Computrabajo traps** (`computrabajo`, Redarbor's general job board at `<cc>.computrabajo.com`, board = the country subdomain, `ar` only so far; live-verified 2026-10-28 from a residential IP and the crawl host):

- **Not Pandapé.** `pandapelatam` is Redarbor's ATS (`<tenant>.pandape.computrabajo.com`); its tenants publish here among thousands of other employers, which is one reason the adapter is `CoverageGated`. Most posters are agencies (ADN, Grupo Gestión, Adecco, Manpower).
- **The edge 403s the project's bare User-Agent** and serves the browser-compatible one (`compatibleUASource`), as on `pandapelatam`. `robots.txt` names no Claude agent; it disallows the sort and filter parameters (`by=`, `pubdate=`, `sal=`, ...) but not `p=`.
- **Every listing stops at page 499** (page 500 answers 404), i.e. ~10k postings, and Argentina holds ~15.2k. So the walk is province by province — the location filter (`ul[data-id=idlocation]`, 25 provinces with their sizes, read from `/ofertas-de-trabajo/?p=2` because page 1 redirects home), the largest (Capital Federal, ~4k) far under the cap. A province running past the cap fails the crawl; a country that big per province would need a finer slice. Past a listing's end a page answers 200 with no card and a hidden `#gtm_afs_elements` of `0`; a cardless page without that marker (a challenge) fails the crawl rather than ending the province. `/empleos-en-remoto` is NOT a province: its postings also sit in their province, so it is not walked. A full Argentine walk is ~770 pages of ~330 KB, ~14 minutes at one a second — the larger part of each run. Any listing page failing fails the crawl, since a partial walk would let the company-scoped sweep close postings it never reached.
- **The card carries everything but the body**: title, employer (the link to its company page), place, and fact spans (`i_salary`; `i_home` for "Remoto", `i_home_office` for "Presencial y remoto"). A card with no company link hides its employer ("Importante empresa del sector", 12.5% of the pool) and is dropped before any request.
- **The body is the posting page's `div[div-link=oferta]`**, not the JobPosting: one page in seven carries no ld+json at all. The block holds fact tags (salary, contract, schedule, arrangement), the text, a "Requerimientos" list ("3 años de experiencia" is the experience), a "Palabras clave" keyword line and skill chips; the last two are left out. When a JobPosting is present it adds `datePosted`, the employer and the country; its `addressLocality`/`addressRegion` are swapped, so the card's place is used. A withdrawn posting redirects to its province's listing (read as gone); a page with neither an offer block nor the listing marker is no answer and the posting waits for the next run.
- **Salary is the card's "$ 2.500.000,00 (Mensual)"**, one figure, pesos, no currency stated. Posters type placeholders ("$ 2.000,00", "$ 11.111,00", "$ 999.999,00"), so a figure under 100,000 or of one repeated digit is not stored, and only on the `ar` board is it named ARS.
- **Shares (2026-10-28):** Informática/Telecom 578 (3.8%), "Remoto" 266 (1.8%). New postings only (`HydratingSource`), at most 600 posting pages a run, budget spent freshest card first (the age line, "Hace 7 horas" — a province lists promoted cards days old ahead of new ones), then by listing rank across provinces, hydration stops 5 minutes before the run's deadline.
