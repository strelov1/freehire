**Bizneo traps** (`bizneo`, the ATS of the Spanish HR suite Bizneo HR, career sites on hosts under `bizneo.cloud`; live-verified 2026-10-27 — 39 hosts in six Common Crawl snapshots, 33 with openings, ~560 postings, the largest `somos-grupo-mas` at 219; ES with some PT/IT/MX/BR tenants):
- **The board is the whole host**, because two shapes coexist: `<company>.careers.ats.bizneo.cloud` and a vanity label (`jobs.abeienergy.bizneo.cloud`, `sivsa.bizneo.cloud`). The older `*.bizneo.com` career hosts (`groundforce.bizneo.com`, `ats.bizneo.com/trabajar/<t>`) answer 404/500/a login page and are not boards. `atsboard` matches the dotted apex `bizneo.cloud` in host mode, which keeps `bizneo.com` out.
- **Some customers serve the same site from their OWN domain** (measured 2026-10-27, PT: `emprego.imancorp.pt` 300+, `trabalho.grupo-crit.com` 279, `opportunities.noesis.pt` 118, `vagas.blcjobs.pt` 27, `recrutamento.sa-formacao.pt` 9; ES: `empleo.imancorp.es`). The pages, the `/jobs?page=N` walk and the card links are identical, so the adapter takes any host as a board except the vendor's `bizneo.com` ones. Such a host is not URL-derivable: `atsdetect.DetectSelfHosted` finds it by the career-site bundle `assets.bizneo.com/vite/assets/custom-companies-*.js` (not by any `bizneo` string — a customer's corporate site can link the vendor's referral page). The harvest prober counts a custom host only when its listing loads that bundle.
- **Listing = `/jobs?page=N`, ~10 cards a page; a page past the end renders no card**, which ends the walk. The card carries title, category, city and the work-mode label, enough for a SeenRefresh.
- **Most posting pages carry NO JobPosting** (1 of 33 sites measured): the body is the `.general-content` block(s) and the side card's fields are `div[title=…]` ("Ubicación", "Modalidad de trabajo", "Jornada laboral"), labelled in the site's language. Without a JobPosting there is no date and no country.
- **The identity is the posting slug.** The card links to nothing else; most slugs end in a UUID, some are bare title slugs and may contain `_`.
- **One edge serves every site and answers 429 to a fast client**: the first crawl (2026-10-27, eight detail workers) lost three listings and 13 posting pages to it, so the provider is paced at 2 requests/s across all boards (`bizneoRequestInterval`).
