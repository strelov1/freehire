**Dice traps** (`dice`; live-verified 2026-11-17 from a residential IP, plain HTTP, no key):

- **Boardless, one host, and NOT a technology-only board.** 111,962 open postings (`totalJobCount` 134,387 with remote). A random 40 of one day's listing held a tax manager, HVAC and building-automation technicians, an employee-relations consultant and a corporate associate beside the engineers, so the adapter sets no `IsTechHint` and leaves technical-or-not to the pipeline's catalogue filter. 1,241 of the 3,480 postings of one day were agency (`employerType: Recruiter`) ones.
- **The listing is the search PAGE, not an API.** `GET https://www.dice.com/jobs?filters.postedDate=ONE&page=N` server-renders its results into the Next.js flight payload: `self.__next_f.push([1,"<chunk>"])` calls whose string chunks, concatenated, hold `"jobList":{"data":[...],"meta":{pageCount,totalResults,...}}`. A row carries `guid` (the id, also the posting URL's tail), `detailsPageUrl`, `companyName`, `title`, `postedDate`/`modifiedDate`, `employmentType` (a comma-joined label, `Full-time`, `Contract, Third Party`), `workplaceTypes` (a list: `On-Site`, `Remote`, `Hybrid`, sometimes several, 3% empty), `salary` (free text) and `jobLocation` (absent on a remote posting, and its `country` is `USA`, `US`, `Canada`, ...). The `summary` is cut at ~500 characters, so the body needs the posting page. The keyed search API behind it (`job-search-api.svc.dhigroupinc.com`, an `x-api-key` header) is not used. A page without `"jobList":{"data":[` fails the run rather than reading as an empty one.
- **robots.txt allows exactly this.** It disallows `/jobs?q*` and `/jobsearch/` (the keyword search, which the adapter never uses) but not `/jobs?filters...`, and `Allow: /jobs` and `Allow: /job-detail` are longer than its `Disallow: /job`, so the posting pages are allowed too. `sitemap-index.xml` names no posting sitemap (company-profile and career-advice ones, last modified 2023). Served under the project's own User-Agent (`freehire/0.1`) and `Go-http-client`.
- **A search stops at page 25: 750 results of 112k, whatever the filter.** Page 26 and beyond answer page 25 again with an empty list, `meta.pageCount` (3,733 unfiltered) notwithstanding. So a run reads a WINDOW, like `builtin`: the postings of the last day (`filters.postedDate=ONE`, 3,480 on 2026-11-17; `THREE` 5,955, `SEVEN` 17,270), cut into slices of at most 750 by the site's own filters, `meta.totalResults` deciding which. The cut order is `employerType` (3 values) then `easyApply` (2), which partition the catalogue exactly (their facet counts sum to the total), then `employmentType` (`FULLTIME`/`PARTTIME`/`CONTRACTS`/`THIRD_PARTY`) and `workplaceTypes` (`On-Site`/`Remote`/`Hybrid`), whose values overlap (a posting can state several; the walk de-duplicates) and which a posting stating none falls out of. The 3,480-posting window took 19 probes to reach leaves of at most 606 (Direct Hire + easyApply + Contract + On-Site). A leaf still over 750 after every cut is walked to page 25 and logged. `filters.isRemote` and `sort`/`sortBy` are accepted and ignored: the sort is always relevance.
- **The listing is not a stable snapshot.** 12 consecutive pages of one slice returned 360 rows but 294 distinct postings: relevance ties reorder between requests, so a page can repeat or skip a posting. The walk de-duplicates by `guid` and a missed posting is re-listed by the next run, which is also why the sweep grace is not short.
- **Hence the 31-day `sweepGrace`.** A posting is re-listed only inside its first day and whenever Dice reposts it; its JobPosting states `validThrough` exactly 31 days after `modifiedDate`. Never `fullCatalog` or `fullBoardListing`. A run is ~120 listing pages for the window plus the slice probes, then at most `diceDefaultMaxNewPerRun` (500) posting pages, at one request per second (`diceRequestInterval`); 40 requests 0.4 s apart and 12 at 1 s were never refused, but nobody has measured a sustained run or the crawl host. A live run on 2026-11-17 (1 s pace, residential IP) listed 2,973 distinct postings of the window in 168 s without a refusal, then hydrated its budget.
- **Coverage-gated** (`FetchNewGated`): a posting whose employer a first-party ATS already covers is yielded body-less and its page never bought.
- **The body is the ld+json JobPosting on the posting page** (`id="jobDetailStructuredData"`): `description` as HTML with `<br />`, `datePosted`, `validThrough`. Its `baseSalary` is ONE figure (140001 for a posting whose listing states `USD 140,001.00 - 180,000.00 per year`) and it states no work arrangement, so salary, arrangement, type and location stay as the LISTING stated them.
- **Salary is employer-typed free text.** Of 360 sampled rows 346 had one, and only the shape Dice renders itself, `USD 35.00 - 38.42 per hour` / `USD 109,464.00 per year`, is read (`diceSalaryLabel`); `120000`, `$$70 - $89`, `USD105000 - USD150000 per annum` and `Depends on Experience` state no period or no currency and set nothing.
- **Work arrangement and type are read only when single.** `workplaceTypes: [Remote, On-Site]` states two arrangements and sets none; `Third Party` in `employmentType` is the employer being an agency, set aside before deciding.
