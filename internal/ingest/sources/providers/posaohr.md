**posao.hr traps** (`posaohr`, a Croatian general job board at <https://www.posao.hr>, boardless; live-verified 2026-10-29 from a residential IP, not yet from the crawl hosts):

- **The site republishes the employment service's vacancies, and those are `hzz`'s.** Of 3,909 cards in the main listing, 3,625 were HZZ's, marked on the card by an `<em>` in the title ("(HZZ oglas)", "(HZZ job ad)") and on the posting page by `h1.hzz_o`/`em.hzz` and a "Podaci su preuzeti sa" link to `burzarada.hzz.hr`. Both are skipped: the card by its marker, a page that turns out to be one by its markers. The text "HZZ" in a body is no sign — an own posting mentions the health insurer HZZO. None of the 278 own posting pages read carried a marker.
- **Not the "nasi" (our ads) filter, and not the main listing.** `robots.txt` disallows `/poslovi/*/` except `/poslovi/stranica/*`, so `/poslovi/vrsta-oglasa/nasi/` is off limits; and both it and the main listing (`/poslovi/stranica/N`) show only 278 of the site's own postings. The county pages (`/zupanije/<county>/stranica/N`, allowed) show 572, among them Hrvatski Telekom, Konzum and PLIVA, which the main listing omits. A county lists every posting filed under it or under "all of Croatia", so the 22 slices (20 counties, the City of Zagreb, `inozemstvo` = abroad) overlap and are merged by id.
- **Every slice sorts the site's own postings before HZZ's**, so a county's walk stops at the first page carrying an HZZ card, or at its last page: ~95 pages for all 22, ~1m45s at one request a second. Measured on two full county walks (342 and 722 cards): no own card after the first HZZ one. A page without the pager ("Stranica 1 od ukupno 21") is not a listing and fails the crawl, as does a cardless page short of the last and any county page failing, since the sweep is company-scoped. The order among postings published together is unstable (a walk repeated ~12 of 284 cards and so missed as many); the 48h sweep grace absorbs it, as every run reshuffles. The employer carousel above the list links postings too, but not as `a > div.search_grid`, which is what a card is.
- **The display language lives in the PHP session**, chosen by the visitor's address when unset (English from a Brazilian one). A run first GETs `/trebam/promijeniti-jezik/hr` on its cookie client, and a posting page not answered in `<html lang="hr">` is no answer. The listing is read by class, not by label.
- **Country is the county slice, structurally**: a posting filed under a Croatian county and not under `inozemstvo` is `hr`; abroad-only postings (170 of 572, mostly Austrian and German staffing agencies — teampool, HSM, Humanus) and the few in both (4) carry no structured country, and their place ("Ulm, Njemačka", "Salzburg, Austrija") goes to the location dictionary.
- **A card carries the title, the employer and the place, never the body**, so the adapter is `CoverageGated`: new postings only, newest id first, 600 posting pages a run at one a second with two workers, stopping 5 minutes before the deadline; the whole catalogue fits one run (~10 minutes cold). The card cuts long places with "..."; a hydrated posting takes the page's.
- **Two page shapes, ~half each** (of 278: 144 and 134). A designed ad is the employer's own HTML in `div.custom` (with a `<style>` the sanitizer drops; every one held 900+ characters of text). A form posting is labelled rows under "O poslu" and "O zaposleniku"; the third section, "Kontakt podaci poslodavca" (address, e-mail, web), is left out. A taken-down posting 302s to the home page (also what a wrong slug does), which is read as gone.
- **Structured facts are few.** Pay is a header line ("od 1 500 € neto", "od 2 453 € bruto"; 111 of 278) naming net or gross but no period, so it leads the body as text, as on `mojposao`. "Vrsta posla" is a multi-select of durations ("stalni radni odnos", "rad na određeno vrijeme") plus "student servis" and "sezonski posao"; only a lone "honorarni posao / freelance" (1) maps, to contract. "Potrebno radno iskustvo" is a number word ("dvije godine", "šest do deset godina"; 65 of 278). A poster ticks up to three categories, so `IsTechHint` is set only when "Informatika i telekomunikacije" is the only one (1 of 278; 3 tick it). No posting date is shown.
