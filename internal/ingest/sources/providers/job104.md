**104 Job Bank traps** (`job104`; live-verified 2026-10-25 from a residential IP, fsn and the crawl host):

- **The board is a 104 AREA CODE, not a category.** A posting has one address (`jobAddrNo`) but up to three job categories (`jobCat`), and the pipeline namespaces a posting's identity by board — category boards would store most postings two or three times. Areas partition the catalogue. A board is a district (`6001001005`), or a whole county/overseas region (`6001023000`, `6003002000`) where that reads in under 100 pages; `job104AreaPrefix` turns the code into the address prefix its postings share.
- **The search serves at most 100 pages of ~30 rows** and its `total` saturates at 1001, so `lastPage < 100` is the only proof a slice was read whole. A board at the cap is split first over the five `jobexp` brackets, which are disjoint (their totals sum to the unfiltered one), and then down the category tree (`static.104.com.tw/category-tool/json/JobCat.json`: 18 families → 44 groups → 619 leaves). Brackets first, because a posting under three categories is FETCHED three times — splitting by category first made the largest districts outlast the run budget. A posting met in several category slices is kept once. Measured over the areas: Taiwan alone is ~12k listing pages per full pass with 57 districts still at the cap, so this provider needs shards.
- **The filters are soft.** Promoted rows from other areas are mixed into a page (an area holding 5 postings answered 11 rows), so a row is kept only when its own `jobAddrNo` lies inside the board's area.
- **The listing row is the whole posting.** Its `description` is the detail endpoint's `jobDescription` verbatim, so a crawl issues no detail requests. Identity is the job code at the end of `link.job` (`/job/8puve`); the row's `jobNo` is a different internal id the detail endpoint 404s on.
- **Salary:** `s10` 10 is "negotiable" (both bounds 0), 30 hourly, 40 daily, 50 monthly, 60 yearly, 70 part-time monthly; `salaryHigh` 9999999 is "and up". Always TWD. `period` is the minimum years of experience PLUS ONE, 0 meaning no requirement. `jobRo` 3 (高階, "senior") is a pay band, not an employment type, and is not mapped.
- **Transport:** both crawl hosts get a custom Cloudflare 403 for a plain client and through BOTH proxies; the Go Chrome-fingerprint client (`fingerprinthttp`) gets the "Just a moment" challenge instead. The Scrapling service's `http` mode (curl_cffi impersonation) is served at ~2-4 s a page, so `job104` is a `scraplingProviders` row. Requests carry a same-origin `Referer`. **The search rate-limits PER ADDRESS, and the shards add up**: the scheduler runs several shards at once, each with its own limiter. From the crawl host's own IP five shards at 0.8 s each (~6/s together) drew 429s (no `Retry-After`) within minutes, and at 4 s each they still drew 1-19 failed boards per shard, while a residential IP was served 200 requests at 0.5 s without one. Through the Scrapling service's ROTATING proxy (`proxy: true`, a new exit per request) 40 of 40 were served at ~3 s a page, so that is the route. Each run paces to one request every 1.2 s (at 2 s, a shard holding two of the largest districts outlasted its budget) and retries a 403/429/5xx/unanswered request with a doubling 5 s backoff (the Scrapling client retries nothing). A full pass is 24 shards under a 4500 s budget on a 16h cadence; with every shard through the rotating proxy, the 2026-10-26 pass drew no 429 at all. `static.104.com.tw` has no such edge and is read on a plain client.
- **`jobCat` under 2007 (資訊軟體系統類) sets `IsTechHint`**; its 37 leaves are all technical crafts. Location is the area table's English name (`Zhongzheng District, Taipei City, Taiwan`), and only a Taiwanese address states a country.
