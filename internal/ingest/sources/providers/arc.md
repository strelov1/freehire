**Arc traps** (`arc`; live-verified 2026-10-30):

- **The complete list needs a signed-in session.** The public `arc.dev/remote-jobs` and `/remote-jobs/<category>` pages render only the newest ~30 external postings each, with no pagination; walking all 664 sitemap pages reached 3,548 of 7,506 (47%). The signed-in site's own search (`SEARCH_EXTERNAL_JOBS` in its bundle) is `POST api.arc.dev/p/job-posts/search` with `{"job_type":"fulltime"|"freelance","page":N,"per_page":100,"sort_by":"latest"}`, and it answers 401 `you can not pass!` without the session cookie. The adapter reads the whole Cookie header from `ARC_SESSION_COOKIE` and is registered only where that is set. A 401/403 fails the crawl and names the variable, so an expired session shows in `board_health` instead of reading as an empty board. Every response sets a fresh cookie that the adapter ignores, so the stored session expires on Arc's schedule, not ours.
- **The two job types are the whole catalogue.** On 2026-10-30, `fulltime` stated 7,148 and `freelance` 358, which together are the public page's `totalExternalJobCount` (7,506). Each walk must read exactly its stated total of distinct keys or the crawl fails (the provider is `fullCatalog`).
- **The listing has no body. The job page has it, and it is Markdown with raw `<strong>` tags.** `arc.dev/remote-jobs/j/<url_string>-<random_key>` is public, and its `__NEXT_DATA__` `props.pageProps.job.description` is turndown-style Markdown (`*   ` bullets, `  \n` hard breaks) that kept LinkedIn's inline `<strong>`. It is rendered with goldmark's unsafe renderer and then sanitized; the default renderer would replace every `<strong>` with a "raw HTML omitted" comment.
- **A posting Arc no longer carries REDIRECTS, it does not 404.** An unknown key answers 308 to the bare `/remote-jobs` listing. Landing anywhere outside `/remote-jobs/j/` is the gone answer; `job.closed: true` is the other one.
- **The apply link is LinkedIn, so the stored URL is Arc's page.** All 33 sampled postings applied through `*.linkedin.com/jobs/view/...`. The listing has no salary for external postings, and neither does the page.
- **`required_countries` turns US states into countries.** Arc derives it from LinkedIn's "City, ST, United States", so `["US","IL"]` is Illinois, not Israel, and `["US","VA"]`, `["US","GA"]` and `["US","CA"]` are the same shape. `GE` and `JE` also appear beside `US`, apparently for Georgia and New Jersey. 866 of 4,300 postings listed `US` beside one of these codes. Such a list is demoted to `FallbackCountries` and stripped of every colliding code (`arcStateCollisions`). A list without `US` is taken as stated.
- **Every posting is remote by the board's own scope**, so `WorkMode` is `remote`. That is Arc's claim, and a page body sometimes says "Hybrid" for some of its locations. `experience_levels` maps only the levels our vocabulary has; `manager`, `director`, `architect` and `vp` state none. `position_type` (engineering/marketing/design) is coarser than our categories and is left to the dictionary.
- **~40% of the catalogue is employers freehire already crawls first-party** (measured 2026-10-30 against prod: 77% of postings at a known company, about half of those already open with us). Hence `CoverageGated`. One request a second for the listing and pages together, and 2,000 new pages a run at most: a cold catalogue fills over four daily runs.
