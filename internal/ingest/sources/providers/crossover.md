**Crossover traps** (`crossover`; live-verified 2026-11-18 from a residential IP, not yet from the crawl host):

- **Boardless aggregator, brand per posting.** crossover.com recruits for Trilogy / ESW Capital, but the live catalogue is mostly Alpha School, GT School, 2 Hour Learning and similar brands (46 active, 2026-11-18), a large share of them on-site campus guides. `Work_Model__c` maps to `WorkMode`; the company is the posting's `Brand__r.Name`. Trilogy roles come and go: the sitemap's ~10,300 `/jobs/<id>/...` URLs are mostly closed ("Not accepting applications"), so it is never the listing.
- **The listing is the SPA's own XHR:** `GET profile-api.crossover.com/pipelines?status=Active`, keyless (a bare `/api/v3/jobs` is 401/403, and `www.crossover.com/api/...` answers the SPA shell). A Salesforce `Product2` dump: `ProductCode` is the id, `DisplayUrl` the public page (a brand subdomain: `alpha.`, `gt.`, `ed.`, `www.`). No posted date.
- **Bodies live in a Kentico Kontent CMS**, read through `kontent-proxy.crossover.com/items` (the public delivery proxy the SPA uses). One request with `system.codename[in]=pipeline_<code>,...` returns every body; sections `hook`, `what_you_will_be_doing`, `responsibilities`, `requirements`, `nice_to_have`. A pipeline with no CMS item (or an empty one) becomes an `unreadableDetail` stub; a failed CMS request fails the crawl.
- **Pay** is `Yearly_Rate__c` in USD (hourly x hours x 50 weeks; 0 on fellowships, which are then left without salary).
- **An empty pipeline list fails the crawl**, so a moved endpoint cannot read as an emptied board and close everything.
