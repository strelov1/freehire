**Infoempleo traps** (`infoempleo`; live-verified 2026-10-27 from a residential IP and the crawl host):

- **Plain HTTP passes; `robots.txt` names no Claude agent** and disallows only search-form URLs (`/busqueda_ofertas_resultados.cfm`, `/trabajo/fecha_*`, `/trabajo/categoria-profesional_*`, …), not `/trabajo/pagina_N/` or the posting pages. There is no sitemap (`/sitemap.xml` is the 404 page).
- **The listing is `/trabajo/pagina_N/`, 20 cards a page, ~390 pages (~7.8k postings) on 2026-10-27.** Past its end the site answers **200 with no cards**, not a 404 (pages beyond ~500 do 404), so the walk stops at the first card-less page. The page also links postings OUTSIDE the cards (a "related" rail, ~4 a page); only `li.job-offer-card` is read, since the card is what names the employer. A later page failing ends the walk with what was read — not `fullCatalog`, nothing states a total.
- **The card carries everything but the body**: title, employer (`p.company-name`), place, a contract line ("Indefinido · Jornada completa", "Prácticas · …", "Autónomo o freelance · …"), an experience line ("Al menos 2 años de experiencia") and, on few postings, a remote line ("Teletrabajo parcial" = hybrid, "Teletrabajo 100%" = remote). So the adapter is `CoverageGated` without a request.
- **The posting page's schema.org JobPosting is the body**: `description` (HTML), `datePosted` (a bare date), `hiringOrganization.name`, `jobLocation` as ONE Place (`addressCountry` "ES"), `baseSalary` in EUR a YEAR on ~a quarter, `employmentType` FULL_TIME/PART_TIME/OTHER, and `jobLocationType: TELECOMMUTE` ONLY on fully remote postings — a hybrid posting's JSON-LD says nothing, its card does. `industry` is the employer's area; "Tecnología e informática" sets `IsTechHint`. A withdrawn posting answers **200 with no JobPosting**, read as gone.
- **Most posters are agencies** (Manpower/Experis, Michael Page, Iman Temporing, Crit and other ETTs); they are filed under the name the posting gives.
- **The run is bounded, and stops short of its deadline.** One limiter paces every request at one a second with two workers; a run reads at most `infoempleoMaxNewPerRun` (900) unseen postings, newest id first, and stops hydrating `infoempleoDeadlineMargin` (5 min) before its context deadline — a hydrating crawl writes nothing until `FetchNew` returns, so a run that hits its deadline stores nothing.
