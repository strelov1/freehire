**Jobnet traps** (`jobnet`, Denmark's public job portal jobnet.dk; live-verified 2026-10-31 from a residential IP):

- **The search serves the first 10,000 results of any query.** With 23,169 ads stated, `PageNumber=100&ResultsPerPage=100` was full and page 101 empty; `ResultsPerPage` above 100 is refused. The old unfiltered walk took that empty page for the end of the catalogue, and its sweep was company-scoped, so on 2026-10-31 17,113 of 28,281 open rows had not been seen for a week: 14,428 of them were gone from the portal (16 of 16 sampled answer 404 on `jobnet.dk/find-job/<id>`), 2,685 were live past the window, and ~10k live ads had never been listed at all.
- **The occupations facet is the partition.** Every ad carries exactly one occupation (880 of them; the facet's counts summed to the stated total), the largest 730 ads, and a repeated `occupations=` parameter is an OR. The crawl reads the facet, packs occupations into slices under the window (`jobnetSliceAds`, at most 100 ids so the URL stays a few KB) and walks each slice to its first empty page: ~240 requests at ~3 s each. The facets are disjunctive — a filtered answer's occupation facet still counts the whole catalogue. Regions do not partition (~2.3k ads in none) and nor do the occupation areas (746 ads in none).
- **Paging is unstable across ties.** Every `OrderType` (`PublicationDate`, `BestMatch`, `ApplicationDate`) orders ads that share the key afresh per request, so a walk lists some ads twice and skips as many: 0.3-1% per walk, a DIFFERENT set each time (three walks of one 2,862-ad slice missed 24, 13 and 18 and together missed one). That random miss is what the 48h sweep window absorbs at a two-hour cadence; a slice that ends a whole page or more short of what it stated fails the crawl.
- **`fullCatalog`.** Any request failing, an empty catalogue, or a facet that does not account for the stated total fails the crawl, so a finished one has listed every ad and jobnet is swept by source.
