**JobStash traps** (`jobstash`; live-verified 2026-10-31):

- **The list endpoint is the whole catalogue.** `/jobs/list?page=N&limit=200` stated `total` 16,963, and a walk read 85 full pages, 16,963 distinct `shortUUID`s, bodies inline. The walk fails on a page that comes back empty before that total, at the page cap, and on a listing with no posting, so a run is the whole feed or an error — which is what earns `fullCatalog`.
- **Most of the feed is ATS-covered, and some employers leave it entirely.** ~80% of a run's postings are skipped as "company already covered by a non-aggregator source". On 2026-10-31, 7,470 of 11,652 open rows had gone unseen for a week. None of them was in the live listing, and 15 of 20 sampled answered gone at the employer's own ATS. `pipeline.CoveredRefresher` (#3963) refreshes the covered rows still listed and puts their companies in the sweep. That still left ~3k rows whose employer had no posting left in the feed, and the company scope never names such an employer again. Only the source-scoped sweep reaches them.
