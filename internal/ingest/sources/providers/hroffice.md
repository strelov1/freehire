**HR Office traps** (`hroffice`, the recruiting module of a Dutch HR suite at `<site>.inhroffice.com`; live-verified 2026-10-25 — 46 site labels in five Common Crawl snapshots, 31 with openings, 560 vacancies, NL 533 / BE 21 / DE 6, the largest `cardia` at 168 and `sawiday` at 105):
- **One open JSON listing on the site's host, `/api/nl/vacancies`** (a bare array: `id`, `title`, `city`, `country` in Dutch, `publishDate` as `02-01-2006`), no body. The body is the server-rendered page `/nl/job/<id>`, in `.vacancy-main-container > section`; that section ends with the "Solliciteren" button block, which is cut.
- **An unknown or switched-off label answers HTTP 500** with a `MiniJobPortalNotFoundException` body (the home page too). The shared client cannot tell that from a real server error, so it stays an ordinary failure and the chronic-board close retires the board.
- **Many employers show the same vacancies on their own domain** (careers.fitforme.com, werkenbij.jaarbeurs.nl). Those sites pull from HR Office and do NOT serve the API, so the board is always the inhroffice.com label; the custom domain names no board. `link.inhroffice.com` is the vendor's shortener, not a tenant.
- **`city` is null on about half the vacancies.** The location is written as the page shows it, "City, Country"; the Dutch country name also goes through `location.NormalizeCountry`.
