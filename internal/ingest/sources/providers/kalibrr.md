**Kalibrr traps** (`kalibrr`; live-verified 2026-10-15):

- **The board is a COUNTRY MARKET, always read through the "IT and Software" job function.** `GET /kjs/job_board/search?country=<Country>&function=IT and Software&limit=&offset=` is the site's own search, keyless, any UA; `robots.txt` disallows only `/root` and `/candidate/profile`. Indonesia held 1,553 postings (249 IT), the Philippines 812 (190 IT). Function and country names are case-sensitive and spelled as the platform spells them.
- **An unknown country or function is IGNORED, not refused.** `country=indonesia`, `country=Thailand` or `function=it-and-software` answer the default market (842 rows, mostly Philippine, every function) with HTTP 200. So every row is checked against the board's country (`google_location.address_components.country`) and the function, and one row outside the slice fails the crawl. Without a `country` the search answers that default mix rather than everything; it is not a board.
- **Offset paging under the default relevance sort repeats and skips rows**: Indonesia paged by 500 came back as 1,420 distinct of 1,553, while one `limit=2000` request returned all 1,553 distinct. A board is therefore ONE request, checked against `count` (exact) and for duplicates, and a board over `kalibrrLimit` fails rather than paging — which is what earns `fullBoardListing`.
- **A row is the whole posting**: `description` + `qualifications` are HTML, so there is no hydration. The canonical page is `/c/<company code>/jobs/<id>/<slug>` (the page's own `rel=canonical`).
- **`apply_redirect_url` is rare and not always the posting's own**: 1 of 249 Indonesian IT rows (BCA's career site), 4 of 190 Philippine ones, all four the same `forms.gle` form. A redirector or a link shared by several postings keeps the Kalibrr page.
- **A hidden salary still carries its figures**: `salary_shown: false` rows can hold `20000000` IDR/month. Only shown ones are published; `salary_interval` is `month` on almost all, with `day`/`hour`/`year` rare.
- **Both `is_work_from_home` and `is_hybrid` false is silence, not on-site**, and is left to the description. `work_experience` (100-500) and `education_level` (200-550) are undocumented codes and are not mapped.
- **The function is the platform's, not a guarantee**: a few rows filed under IT are not IT ("Sales Associate", "Audio Visual Equipment Operator II"); `IsTechHint` follows the dedicated-category-board rule all the same. One transient 502 was seen in ~40 requests; a failed board is simply retried next run.
