**Golang Jobs traps** (`golangjobs`; live-verified 2026-10-18 from a residential IP and from fsn):

- **The site is a client-rendered shell over Supabase, and that table is the only listing.** `sitemap.xml` names five section pages and no posting; the home page is 7 KB with an empty `#root`. The frontend reads `GET <project>.supabase.co/rest/v1/jobs` (PostgREST) with the project's public anon key in `apikey` and `Authorization: Bearer`. A wrong key answers 401 `Invalid API key`. `robots.txt` disallows only `/admin/`.
- **The project URL and anon key are read from the app bundle on every run** — the techinasia shape. The home page names `/assets/index-<hash>.js`, whose Supabase client is built as `new X("https://<ref>.supabase.co","<jwt>")`. A rotated key or a moved project reaches the next crawl by itself; a bundle that stops carrying that construction fails the crawl by name, and a key the backend refuses fails it with the 401. Only a `supabase.co` host is accepted.
- **The whole table is a couple of requests, bodies inline.** The project's max-rows is 1,000 (a larger `limit` is clamped), so the walk advances by the rows it RECEIVED, ordered by `id`. The count comes from the aggregate `select=count` — the only form of it in the body rather than a `Content-Range` header — read before and after the walk; any page failing, a count that moves, an empty table, or a distinct count off the total fails the crawl, which is what earns `fullCatalog`. Archived rows are invisible to the anon key (`is_archived=eq.true` answers `[]`).
- **The board never archives a posting, so age is the only staleness signal.** All 1,199 listed rows on 2026-10-18 were unarchived, going back to 2025-04; only 4 were posted in the last 30 days and 27 in the last 60. Checked against the postings they link to: of the Greenhouse and Lever ones, 8 of 17 posted within 60 days were still open against 18 of 269 older than three months, and every one of 106 Ashby postings was gone. `golangJobsMaxAge` (60 days) drops the rest on the way in, and since the provider is swept by source, a posting that ages past it is closed by the next run.
- **Half the board is ATS copies, all of them on boards we already crawl.** 642 of 1,199 `application_url`s are recognised by `atsboard` (greenhouse 270, ashby 107, lever 96, workday 32, workable 27, …) across 272 distinct boards, 216 of them already in `boards`. Inside the 60-day window it is 14 of 27, every one on a board we crawl, so the aggregator coverage gate discards them. Of the 56 boards we lack, every greenhouse/lever/ashby/workable one answered 404 or zero postings — they are not missing, they are dead.
- **The row's place is a city row, and "Remote" is a city.** A remote posting is filed under the city `Remote` with its hiring scope as the country: a country, a region (`Europe`, `APAC`, `EMEA`) or `Global`. Any other city states no arrangement, so `WorkMode` is left to the description.
- **Salary is yearly by the site's own markup**: the posting page's JobPosting publishes every range with `unitText: "YEAR"`, and the amounts agree (USD 220,000-280,000; INR 15-25 lakh). `salary_currency` defaults to `USD` on every row, so it counts only beside a bound. 229 of 1,199 rows carry one.
- **One job is sometimes re-posted as a second row under the same link** (Happl, 2026-09-08 and 2026-10-06). A link two rows share keeps the Golang Jobs page as `Job.URL`. Every posting is filed as a "Golang developer position" by the site's own page title, so each sets `IsTechHint` and the `go` skill.
