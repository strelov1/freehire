**ClearedJobs traps** (`clearedjobs`; live-verified 2026-11-17 through a local Scrapling service from a residential IP, no proxy):

- **The US board for people holding a security clearance** (defense contractors and agencies: Peraton, SOSi, and the like): **11,456 open postings** (the `/jobs` page's own total), every trade the cleared workforce does, a large share of it IT. In 36 sampled pages: 18 `FULL_TIME` and 18 `OTHER`, 35 in the United States and 1 in South Korea; clearance levels Secret 11, Top Secret / SCI 10, Top Secret / SCI + Polygraph 10, Top Secret 2, Public Trust 1, none stated 2. robots.txt allows `/job/` and the sitemaps and disallows `/search/`, `/xml_feed/` and `/jobtype/*`.
- **A plain Go client gets 403 on every path, robots.txt and the sitemap included**; the Scrapling `http` fetcher (Chrome's TLS fingerprint, no proxy) is served 200 on all of them, 39 of 39 posting pages and ~1.9 s each. Measured from a residential IP; **the crawl host's datacenter IP is not measured**, so the first lever if it is refused is the proxy (`true` in the row), then the browser.
- **No surface lists the whole board.** The `sitemap_active_jobs.xml` shard holds exactly **10,000** URLs sorted by id ascending (1,438,393 to 1,959,852) and `sitemap_active_jobs_2.xml` is **a byte-for-byte copy of it** (the index names both), so the newest ~1.5k postings are missing and the file's `lastmod` runs ~3 weeks behind. The search listing at `/jobs?page=N` is an infinite-scroll Livewire list: **page N returns the first N pages' rows cumulatively (25 a page), and stops growing at page 40**: 1,000 rows, ~4.4 MB; page 41, 458 and 600 are byte-identical to it. Sorted by relevance it is the newest 1,000 (ids 1,947,332 to 1,965,358), and **999 of them are absent from the sitemap**. The adapter reads both, dedups by id, and so sees ~11,000 of 11,456 (~96%); the rest (older postings past neither cut) are never listed. The listing page is best-effort (losing it leaves a stale listing, not a failed crawl); the sitemap is the base (failing it fails the crawl). Because the union is not the whole board a posting that scrolls out of the newest 1,000 reads as unseen, so the adapter is not `fullCatalog` and declares a 14-day `sweepGrace`.
- **The sitemap holds taken-down postings**: 3 of 39 sampled answered **410** (the board's own statement that the posting is gone, which `detail` treats as gone rather than as a failed read). Any other failure, or a 200 page without a JobPosting (a challenge), is no answer and the posting is retried next run. A crawl in which no page at all answered fails.
- **The page's schema.org JobPosting carries everything**: `title`, `description` (HTML; the whole posting, salary and EEO text included), `datePosted`, `validThrough` (+90 days), `employmentType`, `hiringOrganization.name`, `jobLocation.address` (locality, region, country; **a remote posting carries the country alone**) and `identifier`. **`industry` is not an industry but the clearance level** ("Top Secret / SCI + Polygraph"), and nothing in the description states it, so the adapter appends it as a labelled `Security Clearance: <level>` line, which `internal/dict/location`'s clearance dictionary reads into `requires_clearance` and `clearance_levels` (a test walks the five levels the board uses). There is no structured salary or remote flag: pay and "100% remote" are prose, left to the dictionaries. `employmentType` `OTHER` maps to nothing.
- **`datePosted` can lag the page's own "Posted:" line**: one sampled posting (id 1541169) states `datePosted` 2024-09-15 in its JobPosting and "November 14, 2026" in its sidebar, i.e. an employer refresh. The JobPosting is what the adapter reads; if old `posted_at` values turn out to age live postings out, the sidebar line is the alternative.
- **The listing names neither title nor employer**, so nothing is judged before a page is bought and the adapter is not `CoverageGated`; most of the board's employers run an ATS freehire crawls, so a posting the pipeline discards unsaved would be re-bought every run. Pages are picked with `hydrationLanes`: the 900 newest (highest id) plus 300 from a backlog window that moves every hour, at one request a second with three workers (~20 minutes a run), stopping 5 minutes before the deadline; every request is retried on 403/429/5xx. The cold catalogue (~11k postings, ~215 KB a page) takes about ten runs. `is_tech` is NOT hinted: the board is mixed (intelligence, logistics, linguists, finance), so the title dictionary decides.
