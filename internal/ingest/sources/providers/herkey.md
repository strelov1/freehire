**HerKey traps** (`herkey`; live-verified 2026-10-18):

- **The page is a client-rendered shell; the catalogue is the site's own backend.** `www.herkey.com/jobs` carries an empty `pageProps`, and `sitemaps/jobs-1.xml` (8,371 URLs, last written 2026-03) is stale. The frontend lists through `GET api-prod.herkey.com/api/v1/herkey/jobs/es_candidate_jobs?page_no=&page_size=`, which answers 401 without `Authorization: Token <session>`. The session is the frontend's own anonymous one, `GET /api/v1/herkey/generate-session` (`body.session_id`, a JWT good for 30 days), minted once per run. `robots.txt` disallows nothing under `/jobs`.
- **The index is the whole catalogue in four requests.** `page_size=500` is served; `pagination.total_items` was 1,560 and the walk read 1,560 distinct ids. Any page failing, a total that moves mid-walk, or a distinct count off the total fails the crawl, which is what earns `fullCatalog`. Rows are ordered by `modified_on`, newest first.
- **The index keeps expired postings.** 332 rows carry a `modified_on` from 2025-11 or earlier; their posting resource (`GET /api/v1/herkey/jobs/jobs/<id>`, keyless) says `status: "inactive"` with an `expires_on` months past. They are JobsPikr-scraped copies (`ats_type: jobspikr_external_jobs`) and Talent.com links. The posting resource decides: anything not `active` is dropped. A dropped posting is never stored, so each run re-asks for these; the budget below bounds that. A stored posting that later expires stays open while the index lists it, since a seen posting costs no detail request.
- **Most bodies are only on the posting resource.** 1,495 of 1,560 listing rows carry no `requirements`/`responsibilities`; the body is the resource's `description` (+ those two). Imported pharma bodies are newline plain text and are rebuilt into paragraphs. 10 of 65 sampled pharma postings read the literal `"TBD"`; such a posting is dropped as having no description. An unknown id answers HTTP 400.
- **The posting resource is slow: 4-15 s a request**, measured sequentially. The provider rides the 45 s long-timeout client, four detail workers and a 1 req/s limiter, and hydrates at most 600 postings a run, so a cold start (~1.5k) accretes over three runs instead of outliving its unit.
- **Not an IT board, and a third of the rest is ATS copies.** 1,021 of 1,560 are one pharma employer (Cadila Pharma, field sales and plant roles, from its own careers site). `classify.IsTech` accepts 99 titles (Aristocrat, Ericsson, Credit Karma, GoDaddy, UnitedHealth...). 186 of the 539 others link to greenhouse, smartrecruiters, workday, taleo, lever, workable or zoho, so the adapter is `CoverageGated`. `redirect_url` is Job.URL when it is the posting's own: recruitics' `rx_url` redirect is unwrapped to Aristocrat's Workday posting, and a link two postings share keeps the HerKey page. 151 rows are HerKey-native and have no link.
- **Structured fields are form picklists with defaults.** `work_mode` (`work_from_home`/`work_from_office`/`hybrid`) is set on ~130 rows; `job_types` spells `full time` and `full_time`, and `returnee_program` is a programme flag, not a schedule. `min_year`/`max_year` default to 0-2 (1,092 rows), so only a minimum above zero is mapped. `location_name` is one city or `Any` (no place). Timestamps are zone-less India time. `skills` is free text and is not mapped.
