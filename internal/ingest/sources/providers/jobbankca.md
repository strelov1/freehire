**Job Bank traps** (`jobbankca`; live-verified 2026-11-22 from the crawl host through the Scrapling tier):

- **Canada's national job board, ~67,047 open postings** (the listing's own count), every trade and province. Only part is posted by employers on Job Bank; the rest comes from partner boards named on each posting (Talent.com, indeed.com, CareerBeacon, Québec emploi, Jobs.gc.ca, CivicJobs.ca, SaskJobs, 86network). In the first 75 listing rows 15 were direct ("Job Bank"), 18 Talent.com, 18 indeed.com, 16 CareerBeacon. Every posting, whatever its origin, has its own page, `/jobsearch/jobposting/<id>`, with the same markup. The French site (`guichetemplois.gc.ca`) shares the ids; only the English one is read.
- **robots.txt is `User-agent: *` and `Crawl-delay: 5`, with no Disallow.** The listing, the posting pages and the sitemap-less site are all allowed; the crawl-delay is the budget. Every request of a run, listing or posting page, is paced to one every 5 s (`jobbankcaRequestInterval`). There is no sitemap (`/sitemap.xml` is a 404).
- **The open-data dataset is not an option.** open.canada.ca publishes "Job Postings Advertised on Canada's National Job Bank Website" under the Open Government Licence, but monthly (the October 2026 file appeared on 2026-11-04), as a ~52 MB UTF-16 tab-separated CSV with no description column, and a month stale at best. It is useful for statistics, not for a live board.
- **A plain Go client cannot reach the site from the crawl host: `curl` to `robots.txt` timed out after 20 s.** The Scrapling `http` fetcher, no proxy, served the listing, 20 of 20 posting pages and `robots.txt`. So the row is `http`, `proxy: false` (`scraplingProviders`).
- **The listing is the site's own search, `/jobsearch/jobsearch?searchstring=&sort=D&page=N`: 25 rows a page, newest first, and it pages to the end** (page 2000 served 25 rows, page 2700 a 200 with none). Rows hold only a link (`a.resultJobItem`, `/jobsearch/jobposting/<id>;jsessionid=...?source=searchresults`), a title, the employer, a place and sometimes a salary; the adapter reads the id alone and takes everything else from the posting page. A walk of the whole listing is ~2,700 requests, 3.7 hours at the pace, so the adapter does not make one (below).
- **The posting page is RDFa, not ld+json.** `property` attributes carry `title`, `datePosted` ("Posted on November 22, 2026"), `hiringOrganization` > `name` (inside a `<strong>`, or an external `<a>` followed by a screen-reader note, "This link will open in a new window", which is dropped), `address` > `addressLocality`/`addressRegion` (one per address; a "Various locations" posting has several, and the region reads ", QC" with a comma of its own), `baseSalary` (`minValue`/`maxValue` in `content` attributes WITH thousands separators — "87,658.18" — and `unitText` HOUR/YEAR), `employmentType` ("Permanent employment" over "Full time"), `validThrough` ("2026-12-13") and `description`. A fixed wage states `minValue` alone. Some postings carry no `address` at all, only a label ("Montréal Island") beside the "Location" heading.
- **The body is a hidden `description` span, and its form depends on the partner.** Direct postings and most partners send plain text (a direct posting's structured sections flattened into sentences); Talent.com sends escaped HTML (`&lt;p&gt;...`), which `jobbankcaBody` renders as markup after one unescape. Partner descriptions are cut at ~4,000 characters. **One in twenty has no body at all** (86network answers an empty span): such a posting is not yielded, and is asked for again while it sits in the listing window.
- **"Work location" reads "On site" on every partner posting by default**, so only `Hybrid` and `Remote` are used as a work mode; the posting's `detail` states it in an `li` labelled "Work location".
- **Titles are lower case** ("child caregiver - private home") as the site spells them.
- **The `?` for an apostrophe or dash in a partner body is the site's own encoding loss**, not the adapter's.
- **Coverage is a window, by design.** 5 s a request is 720 requests an hour against ~2,200 new postings a day, so one run reads the newest `jobbankcaListingPages` (160, ~13 min) listing pages and hydrates at most `jobbankcaFreshPerRun` + `jobbankcaBacklogPerRun` (300 + 50, ~29 min) unseen postings, newest first, stopping 5 minutes before the run's deadline (a hydrating crawl stores nothing until `FetchNew` returns). The cold catalogue (~67k) is not walked: the catalogue fills as postings pass through the window, and a posting lives 7-30 days (`validThrough`), so it holds the live board after about a month. At four runs a day the hydration budget (1,400 a day) is two thirds of the intake, so the unhydrated third are the oldest of each window; raise the cadence, or the per-run bounds with a longer unit timeout, to close the gap.
- **The sweep grace is 35 days** (`jobbankcaSweepGrace`): a stored posting stops being refreshed a couple of days after it appears, because it leaves the window, and closing it on the 48-hour default would empty the catalogue. A posting past its `validThrough` is hidden by the deadline rather than by a close.
- **The classifier gap should be small for English, but the board is not IT.** Titles are plain English; the board is dominated by care, trades, retail and hospitality, so only a small share (low single-digit percent) resolves a technical category. `is_tech` is NOT hinted: the board states nothing of the kind.
