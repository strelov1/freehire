**Tencent traps** (`tencent`; live-verified 2026-10-15):

- **careers.tencent.com is the mainland-China experienced catalogue, and it re-lists Tencent's Workday.** Of 804 postings in the 技术 (Technology, `parentCategoryId=40001`) category, 96 carry `SourceID: 4` and a `PostURL` on `tencent.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com` — Tencent's international tenant, which the `workday` adapter already crawls (245 open postings under `company_slug=tencent`). They are skipped; the 708 `SourceID: 1` rows are all in China. **Every campus posting here is such a copy**: the 11 graduate (`attrId=2`), 48 intern (`3`) and 6 青云计划 (`5`) postings are all `SourceID: 4`. Mainland campus hiring is a separate site (join.qq.com), not crawled.
- **Keyless JSON, any User-Agent, no `robots.txt`** (the path 302s to the site's 404 page). `GET /tencentcareer/api/post/Query` honours `pageSize=2000`: the category is ONE request, checked against `Count` and for duplicate ids, which earns `fullBoardListing`; a category over 2,000 fails rather than paging. The whole site is 2,230 postings (1,908 native).
- **The listing has the responsibilities, not the requirements.** `GET /tencentcareer/api/post/ByPostId?postId=` adds `Requirement`, a team `Introduction` and the sub-category (`OuterPostTypeID`), so the adapter hydrates new postings only (`HydratingSource`). An unknown id answers HTTP 500 `{"Code":500,"Data":"E1005"}`; a failed detail defers the posting. 120 details at 8 concurrent answered 200 with no refusal; a cold run is ~710 requests, ~1 minute.
- **`language=en-us` translates the facets, not the posting**: country, city and category come back in English ("China", "Shenzhen"), the title and body stay as written (Chinese). `LastUpdateTime` is "October 15,2026" in en-us (`2026年10月15日` in zh-cn) and is the only date stated. A listing row names ONE city even when the title names several ("（深圳/北京）").
- **The Technology category is the tech scope, because `classify` cannot read the titles**: over the 708 native technology titles it resolved a category for 5, `IsTech` for 2 and `ConfirmedNonTech` for 0; over the other ten categories (1,200 titles) it resolved 3 categories and confirmed nothing non-technical. The category sets `IsTechHint`; of its sub-categories only Quality Control (`qa`) and Security (`security`) name one discipline.
