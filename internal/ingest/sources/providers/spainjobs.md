**SpainJobs.io traps** (`spainjobs`; live-verified 2026-10-27 from a residential IP):

- **An English-language board that gathers Spanish employers' own career-page postings** and rewrites each in English (Spanish under `/es/`; the page says "This job advert was originally written in Spanish"), with the employer's own apply link. 92,666 postings across ~3.5k employers on 2026-10-27, ~2k new a day. `robots.txt` allows everything but `/api/`, search and `_rsc` URLs, names no Claude agent and sets `Content-Signal: ai-input=yes`. Plain HTTP passes.
- **The value is the vanity domains.** In a 41-posting sample ~60% of apply links pointed at an employer's own domain (careers.adamfoods.com, empleo.imancorp.es, randstad.es, iese.edu), which no ATS adapter can reach; the rest are Workday/Teamtailor/Workable/SmartRecruiters copies of boards we mostly crawl already (the harvest of its apply links found 94% of those boards in the catalogue).
- **The sitemaps are the listing**: `sitemap.xml` names `jobs-new.xml` and `jobs-0..N.xml` (40,000 URLs each) beside company/landing/tag sitemaps. A posting URL is `/companies/<employer-slug>/<original-title-slug>--<hex id>`; the id is the identity, and the employer slug is what makes the adapter `CoverageGated` without a request. No id order exists, so a run reads the most recently modified (`lastmod`) first.
- **The page's schema.org JobPosting is the posting on ~90% of pages**: English `title`, HTML `description` (the full rewritten advert), `datePosted`, `hiringOrganization.name`, `jobLocation` (Place with `addressCountry`), `jobLocationType: TELECOMMUTE` on remote ones, `employmentType` as a string OR an array, `experienceRequirements.monthsOfExperience`, and `baseSalary` as a range OR a single `value` (EUR a YEAR; hourly rates are left in the body). **~10% of pages — mostly staffing agencies' adverts — carry no JobPosting**; those are read from the page itself: the `h1`, the employer from `<title>` ("<role> at <employer> (<city>) | SpainJobs.io"), the body from `#job-advert`. Without that fallback they would be re-bought every run.
- **`Job.URL` is the employer's apply link** (the external `<a>` whose text is "Apply"), as wttj does; the SpainJobs page is the fallback.
- **A page is ~450 KB.** One limiter paces every request at one a second with two workers; a run reads at most `spainjobsMaxNewPerRun` (1,500) unseen pages, stopping 5 minutes before its deadline.
