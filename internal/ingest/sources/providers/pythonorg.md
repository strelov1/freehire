**Python.org traps** (`pythonorg`; live-verified 2026-10-15):

- **The RSS feed is not the catalogue.** `/jobs/feed/rss/` carries bodies but only the newest 20 postings, against 28 live. The listing is the server-rendered `/jobs/` (25 cards a page): page 2 held 3 cards and `?page=3` answered **404**, which the "which page failed decides" walk reads as the end. Not `fullCatalog`: nothing states a total to check the walk against. `robots.txt` disallows nothing under `/jobs/`, and the board answers our own User-Agent.
- **No schema.org JobPosting.** The posting page's only ld+json is the site-wide `WebSite` block, so the body is `div.job-description`; its leading "Job Title" section repeats the title and is dropped. Title, employer, place and date come from the listing card (`span.listing-company-name` holds the link, then the employer as bare text after a `<br>`, with a "New" badge span on recent cards).
- **The "Web" contact link is not an apply URL.** Of the 28 postings, 13 point it at the employer's homepage, 3 at one recruiter's personal LinkedIn profile, one at an Adzuna copy; only 2 named an ATS freehire crawls (one Ashby, one JazzHR `applytojob.com`). Job.URL is therefore always the python.org posting page, and the board's ATS overlap is too small to justify a coverage gate — a cold run is 30 requests in about a second.
- **The "Restrictions" flags are form defaults, not facts.** "No telecommuting" sits on a posting whose location reads "Warsaw (fully remote), Poland"; it is left in the body and not mapped to a work mode. The location is free text ("Remote, Remote/Worldwide, Remote/Worldwide", "100% Remote, USA Only") and is left to the location dictionary. Agencies post too ("mindIT HR Agency (on behalf of a confidential client)") and are stored under that name.
- **Every live posting was filed under "Developer / Engineer"**, the board is Python-only, so `IsTechHint` is set. No metering seen over ~70 requests at our UA, a full 8-way concurrent crawl included.
