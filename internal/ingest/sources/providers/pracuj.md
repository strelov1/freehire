**Pracuj.pl traps** (`pracuj`; live-verified 2026-10-29 from the crawl host):

- **Poland's largest general board** (Grupa Pracuj): ~62.9k open offers in ~48.6k postings — a posting offered in N cities is N offers with one `groupId`, and the `groupId` is the identity. Every trade; ~4.6k postings sit in the two IT categories (5015 "IT - Administracja", 5016 "IT - Rozwój oprogramowania"). ~1.6-1.9k new postings a day. robots.txt names no AI crawler. **theprotocol.it is Grupa Pracuj's IT board and carries the same postings** (4 of 5 sampled theprotocol titles were found on pracuj.pl under the same employer), so it is not crawled separately.
- **Cloudflare refuses a plain client on every path, robots.txt included.** The Scrapling tier's impersonating fetcher is served from the crawl host's own IP (`http`, no proxy, no Webshare traffic). Its `CurrentOffers` sitemap is frozen in 2021 and useless; the listing is the search page itself.
- **The search is server-rendered**: `/praca?cc=<categories>&rop=100&pn=<n>`, results in `__NEXT_DATA__` → `props.pageProps.dehydratedState.queries[]` under the key `jobOffers` (`groupedOffers`, `groupedOffersTotalCount`), newest first. `rop` is honoured up to 100 (~1.2 MB a page). **The server render sometimes answers 200 without the `jobOffers` query**: on the UNFILTERED search about half the pages past ~100 came back that way (two more attempts rarely helped), while searches of up to ~21k postings answered 560 pages without a single miss. So the walk is four category slices (the `cc` filter is a union, IT alone as one of them), each page retried on a missing query, and any page that never answers fails the crawl. Postings filed under categories of two slices (~15%) are deduplicated by `groupId`; one the IT slice lists carries `IsTechHint`.
- **The offer page** (`/praca/<slug>,oferta,<partitionId>`, ~470 KB, the slug is cosmetic) carries the posting under the `jobOffer` query: `sections[]` (bullets, multi-paragraph and dictionary models, plain text — rendered to HTML here), `attributes.employment` and `attributes.workplaces`. Names are in the POSTING's language (an English posting's page says "B2B contract"), so only ids and codes are matched. **A gone posting answers 200 with `isArchive`/`isWithdrawn` true or `publicationDetails.isActive` false**, any id resolving.
- **Pay is per contract** (`typesOfContracts[].salary`: `from`/`to`, `currency.code`, `timeUnit.id` 0 month / 1 hour, `salaryKind.code` `gross` or `net-plus-vat`). ~34% of postings state one. The salary fields take one figure — the contract of employment's (id 0) gross if stated, else another gross one, else B2B's (id 3) net — PLN only; every contract's pay is also written at the top of the body, so which contract and tax basis a figure was is not lost.
- Work mode codes `home-office` / `hybrid` / `full-office` (the listing's labels "Praca zdalna" / "Praca hybrydowa (do uzgodnienia)" / "Praca stacjonarna"), the most remote one winning; `entirelyRemoteWork` also means remote; "Praca mobilna" (field work) means nothing. Offer-level shares: on-site 66%, hybrid 24%, remote 8%; in IT remote 27%, hybrid 65%. Position levels map by id (intern 1, junior 17, mid 4, senior 18, expert 19 → senior); a posting stating two levels gets none. An offer abroad names its country in the posting's language ("Niemcy"), so only an English page's resolves.
- **Cloudflare rate-limits the OFFER pages, separately from the listing.** The first production run read 114 offer pages at about one a second, then every offer request answered 429 "Access denied" for about an hour while the listing kept answering. 170 offer pages at one every ~7.3 s drew no refusal, which rules out any "~100 per window" rule with a window over ~12 minutes; so offer pages go one at a time, one every 8 s, and the first 429 ends the run's buying (asking again only prolongs the block). A run refused its very first offer page fails.
- One run walks ~560 listing pages (~10 min at one request a second, two in flight) and buys at most 360 offer pages, newest first, CoverageGated (the listing names the employer), stopping 5 minutes before the deadline; the schedule gives it a 4,500 s timeout (65 minutes of crawl after cmd/ingest's 10-minute sweep reserve) and a 2 h cadence, ~4.3k offer pages a day against ~1.7k new postings.
