**Remote100k traps** (`remote100k`; live-verified 2026-11-24; 1093 `/remote-job/<slug>` URLs in the sitemap):

- **Boardless aggregator over the public sitemap.** `https://remote100k.com/sitemap.xml` is one flat `<urlset>` (no lastmod): `/remote-job/<slug>` entries are postings, the rest are `/remote-jobs/*` category, `/remote-companies/*`, blog and legal pages. robots.txt disallows only `/admin/`, `/r1/admin/`, `/api/` and `/health/`. There is no listing API and no pagination to walk, so the sitemap IS the full catalogue. Plain GET with a browser UA answers 200; no rate limit seen (27 pages at 0.3 s).
- **The page's schema.org JobPosting is the whole posting**: `title`, `description` (HTML), `datePosted` (date at 00:00 UTC), `employmentType`, `hiringOrganization.name`, `jobLocationType: TELECOMMUTE`, `baseSalary` (MonetaryAmount USD/CAD/..., YEAR, min/max). There is NO `jobLocation` and NO `validThrough`; where the work may be done from is `applicantLocationRequirements` (one Country by name), read through the shared `sitemapBoard.applicantCountries` switch. A page taken down is expected to 404 and is not yielded.
- **Identity is the slug** (`<company>-<title>`), also the JobPosting `identifier.value`; a retitled posting would be a new one.
- **The employer's apply link** is the "Apply for This Job" anchor (`?ref=remote100k` appended), kept as `ExternalApplyURL` for dedup; the canonical `URL` stays the remote100k page.
- Shared body in `sitemapboard.go`. One request per second, two in flight, at most 1500 new pages per run. Needs a `boards` row (`cmd/add-board --provider=remote100k`, no board id) to be scheduled.
- **The site never retires a posting** (checked 2026-11-25): every page answers 200 and stays in the sitemap. Of the 357 open postings past 90 days, the employer's ATS had closed 183 (Instacart, HubSpot, Pinterest, Dropbox among them), kept 104, and 70 could not be checked. So the adapter drops postings whose `datePosted` is older than 90 days (`sitemapBoard.maxAge`) and re-reads every stored page each run instead of refreshing it by identity, since only the page states the date. A stored posting dropped that way is left out of the run, and the stale sweep closes it by source (`fullCatalog`), so an employer whose every posting aged out is closed too; one unreadable page withholds that close for the run. A run reads all ~1.1k pages (~20 min at one request per second).
