**Trudvsem traps** (live-verified 2026-10-30):

- **Without `fullBoardListing` nothing closed it.** Every run hits `cmd/ingest`'s crawl deadline, and a truncated run closes through the board scope only, which needs the marker. The last close was 2026-10-16. By 2026-10-30 the catalogue held 683k open postings against the API's 503k, and 15 of 20 sampled postings unseen for a week were gone from the API. The adapter earns the marker by failing a region on any page error instead of keeping the pages it read, and by splitting past the page ceiling.
- **One query reaches 10k postings; six regions hold more.** The offset is a page index, pages stop at 100 rows, and page 100 answers 500. A region past that is read in date windows: the next query adds `modifiedTo` at the oldest `date_modify` read so far plus `trudvsemWindowOverlap`, and repeats until a window ends under the ceiling. The listing is newest-first by `date_modify` but only roughly (adjacent rows were a second out of order), which is why the bound overlaps.
- **A page takes ~6 s of server time whatever the load.** Six requests held in flight for a minute answered at 6.7 s on average with no errors. The in-flight cap (`trudvsemMaxInFlight`) is therefore throughput, not politeness: at 2 the run reached ~11 of 90 regions, and the large regions were the ones caught at the deadline every run.
