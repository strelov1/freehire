**JobTeaser traps** (`jobteaser`; live-verified 2026-10-28 from a residential IP and the crawl host):

- **The board is a posting LANGUAGE, the URL prefix** (`fr`, `de`, `en`, `da`, `nl`, `es`). The job-ads sitemaps (`assets-cf.jobteaser.com/sitemaps/job_ads_sitemap*.xml`, 7 files) name 31,700 postings, each once, under its primary language: fr 14,582, de 9,448, en 4,551, da 1,687, nl 1,129, es 303. The alternate-language links are the same posting, so languages partition the catalogue. Any job-ads sitemap failing fails the crawl. robots.txt allows everything.
- **Internships, work-study and first jobs at large employers** (KPMG, Capgemini, BNP Paribas, L'Oréal, VINCI). Many of them run ATSs freehire already crawls, which is what the gate is for. ~16% of the French URLs spell a technical title.
- **The URL spells the employer and the title** (`/fr/job-offers/<uuid>-<employer>-<title>`), but nothing marks where one ends. The companies sitemap names 874 French company slugs, and the longest leading match resolves the employer of 68% of the French postings. Those are `CoverageGated` before the page is bought; the rest are bought blind and picked with `hydrationLanes`: 300 freshest plus 100 from a moving backlog.
- **The pages sit behind a Cloudflare challenge.** A plain client gets 403. The Scrapling `http` fetcher was served 1 of 5 direct and a third to four in five over the rotating proxy, and every refusal still costs ~225 KB of proxy traffic. The `browser` fetcher passed 18 of 18 from the crawl host's own IP, at ~8 s a page once the first has paid ~110 s for the challenge. So the pages are a browser `scraplingProviders` row, three in flight, while the sitemaps (a CDN with no challenge) stay on a plain client. A page is ~300 KB, and an unknown id answers 404.
- **The JobPosting's `jobLocationType` is TELECOMMUTE on every page sampled, on-site ones included**, so it is not read. The page's own fact lines are read instead, by `data-testid`: `...CandidacyDetails__RemotePolicy` ("Partiel" = hybrid, "Pas de télétravail" = on-site, "Non spécifié" = nothing) and `...CandidacyDetails__Contract`, whose first word is the contract (CDI, CDD, Stage, Alternance, "VIE / VIA"). `baseSalary` appears on a few postings (`MONTHLY` for work-study); the page's free-text "Salaire" line is not read.
