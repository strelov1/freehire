**Welcome to the Jungle traps** (live-verified 2026-10-15):

- **The board is a profession SUB-CATEGORY reference**, not a country and not the whole index. The index holds ~90.6k postings (60k with a French office, 18k US, 5.2k GB); its `tech-engineering-3NjUy` category ~19.6k, split into sub-categories that partition it — `software-web-development-iMzA4` 7.5k, `data-business-intelligence-yZjY1` 3.9k, `network-engineering-and-administration-yZjhm` 1.7k, `cloud-computing-and-devops-mZTM2` 870, `cybersecurity-2MDhm` 800, `it-support-and-services-3Njcy` 384, `hardware-engineering-lMWZl` 272. `engineering-disciplines-4YWVi` (3.7k) is mechanical/civil engineering. **Since 2026-10-28 every one of the 179 sub-categories is a board**, not just these seven, since the catalogue is broader than IT. A hit carries ONE `sub_category_reference` (a string, never a list), and the sub-category facet counts sum to the index total (90,176 of 90,188), so the boards still partition and no posting is stored under two boards. A posting re-filed under another sub-category moves board, and its old copy is closed by the board-scoped sweep. A reference is self-checking: one that matches nothing FAILS the crawl, because this adapter is swept board-scoped and a renamed reference must not read as a board whose postings all closed.
- **The listing is WTTJ's own Algolia index, and the key is fetched, not configured.** `/api/env` publishes the app id and search key as a `window.env = {...}` script; the key is referer-locked. CloudFront 403s a bare `Mozilla/5.0` User-Agent on that path while the project UA and a full browser UA are served.
- **Algolia reads at most 1,000 hits per query, and `distinct` is configured on the index.** Paging past 1,000 is an error, so the walk partitions a board by `published_at_timestamp` into half-open windows of at most 1,000 and checks each window's hit count against its exact `nbHits`, and the windows' union against the board's unwindowed count — which is what earns `fullBoardListing`. Every query sends `distinct=false`: with it on, a 7,577-posting board answers 1,531.
- **The hit carries no description**, so the adapter is a `HydratingSource` over `api.welcometothejungle.com/api/v1/organizations/<org>/jobs/<slug>` (~5-40 KB), whose `description` + `profile` + `recruitment_process` are the body and whose `apply_url` is the employer's own (present on 91-95% of the two boards smoked, though some of those point at WTTJ's own `ats.welcometothejungle.com`; a posting without one keeps the WTTJ page). The API is keyed by slugs and the index by uuid, so `wttj_reference` must equal the hit's `objectID`. A few postings (4 of 7,557) carry an empty body and are deferred, not stored body-less.
- **About a third of a technical board applies through an ATS we already crawl** (greenhouse, ashby, smartrecruiters, lever, workday, workable — 1,000 data postings sampled), so the adapter is `CoverageGated`. The rest is real reach: Thales, Capgemini, NTT Data, CGI, Safran and the WTTJ-native employers have no first-party board here.
- **No metering observed**: 1,000 posting-API requests at 16-way and a full 7,557-posting board in 8 minutes answered no 429, only 16 client timeouts; the fan-out stays at the shared 8. A first crawl of the seven boards hydrates ~15k postings; steady state is one request per new posting.
