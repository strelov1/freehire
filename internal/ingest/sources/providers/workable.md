**Workable traps** (`workable`):

- **`CompanyDescriber` fetches its OWN `apply.workable.com/api/v1/widget/accounts/{board}` endpoint again but WITHOUT `?details=true`** — that flag only controls whether each job's own full body is inlined, so dropping it shrinks the response substantially (~11x on a sampled board) while leaving the account-level `description` field unaffected, so the second request is cheap rather than a duplicate of `Fetch`'s own (potentially much larger) payload. A first design spike sampled only two large, atypically-run boards (GitLab, Canva) and found the field empty on both, but a follow-up spike against 40 boards drawn at random from the production catalog found it filled on 32 (80%). It is sanitized through `sanitizeHTML` with no `html.UnescapeString` (served as raw HTML).
- **The description fetch was half its traffic.** Until 2026-10-19 the pipeline asked for it on every run, which measured as half of Workable's ~63k requests a day and half of its 18,520 refusals in two days; `CompanyDescriptionDue` now gates it.
- **The 429 that taught the host hold** (`hostbackoff.go`), measured 2026-10-18 on workable (direct IP + refusal-retry proxy, which returns on the second refusal with no wait at all): once its budget tripped ~30 minutes into a run, the rest of the fleet (~1,500 boards) failed inside three minutes at ~1,000 boards a minute, 18,520 refusals in two days (9,311 board listings, each a recorded board failure, and 9,303 company-description fetches).
- **Empty board** (`emptyBoardListing`): account object with `"jobs":[]`; an unknown board 404s.
