**HZZ traps** (`hzz`, the Croatian Employment Service's job exchange burzarada.hzz.hr; live-verified 2026-10-28 from a residential IP):

- **The listing is the 21 county RSS feeds, `/rss/rsszup{1..21}.xml`.** Together they named 7,495 distinct vacancies (7,133 open ones, see below), the same set the 20 category feeds `rsskat1001..1020` name minus `rsszup1000`, "Poslovi u EU" — 128 vacancies ABROAD, which are not read. ~500 vacancies are published each weekday, ~20 on a weekend day. Any county feed failing fails the crawl: the sweep is company-scoped and a missing county would close its employers' other vacancies.
- **Every request needs the ASP.NET session cookie.** The first response 302s to `?AspxAutoDetectCookieSupport=1` and sets it; without a jar the redirect loops. Hence `cookieSessionSource`. `robots.txt` answers 404.
- **The feeds answer 406 to `Accept: application/xml`** (and to `text/html`, `application/rss+xml`): they are served as `text/xml` and the server negotiates strictly. They are also ISO-8859-2. Both broke `GetXML` for every caller, so `GetXML`/`GetXMLResolved` send `application/xml, text/xml` (also needed by Dualoo) and now decode the charset the XML declaration names.
- **An item with an EMPTY `<title />` is USUALLY an open vacancy** (362 of 7,495; 34 of 40 sampled were live, 6 answered the expiry page). It is listed, its title read from the page, and it cannot be judged by the catalogue filter before that page. An expired one is "gone" and is re-asked each run while the feed carries it (~50 pages a run).
- **The feed's description is TRUNCATED mid-word and names no employer.** The employer and the whole body come from the print page `RadnoMjesto_Ispis.aspx?WebSifra=<id>` (~35-57 KB), read for new vacancies only, newest id first, 1,200 a run at one request a second, stopping 5 minutes before the deadline. The page's fields are ASP.NET spans with stable ids (`ctl00_MainContent_lbl…`); the body is `lblOpisPosla` (plain text, when filled) plus the free HTML between the `Ostale informacije` label and the next `<hr>`. An expired or unknown id answers HTTP 200 with "Traženi oglas je istekao!" and no field spans; that is "gone", anything else without the employer span is an error.
- **Employer names are full register names** ("EUROTIM d.o.o. za proizvodnju i trgovinu", "CONER društvo s ograničenom odgovornošću za …"), and a craft (obrt) carries its owner's name and home address. `hzzCompanyName` shortens a spelled-out legal form, cuts the registered activity after it, and reduces a craft to its quoted trading name or the part before the first comma. The page itself truncates long names at ~150 characters.
- **Structured facts:** `Bruto plaća` ("1050 €", 3 of 15 sampled) is the monthly gross pay in euros the form asks for, kept between 500 and 20,000; "pripravnik" in the contract kinds is a traineeship; "Puno radno vrijeme" full time, stated weekly hours part time. "Na određeno" (fixed term) is not contract work and is not mapped. `Način rada` names shifts, never remote work, and "rad od kuće" appears in no feed item: the exchange is ~0% remote.
- **The IT category feed (`rsskat1001`, 88-89 vacancies, ~1.2%) sets `IsTechHint`**; its failing costs only that mark. No employer is known before the page, so only the catalogue filter's title verdict can spare a page (`CoverageGated` with `Covered` unused).
