**InfoJobs traps** (`infojobs`, infojobs.net, Spain; live-verified 2026-10-30 from the crawl host through the Scrapling tier's browser over the proxy):

- **The whole-site listing cannot be walked to its end.** `/ofertas-trabajo?page=N` stated 63,729 offers over 3,187 pages and served pages 1-2999; page 3000 onwards answers an error page (`hasError`) with no offers. The old walk also computed its page count from page 1's 22 cards (two are promoted; every later page has 20) and stopped at ~2.9k, so the oldest ~5.8k live offers were never listed and never refreshed.
- **The categories are the listing.** Page 1's `aggregation.category` lists 21 categories whose counts summed EXACTLY to the stated total, the largest ("Comercial y ventas") 7,328 offers, 367 pages. Each is walked as `?categoryIds=<id>&page=N` to its own `navigation.totalPages`; the crawl fails unless the counts sum to the total, unless each category page echoes its filter in `search.categoryIds` — the semantic `/ofertas-trabajo/<slug>` path silently serves the UNFILTERED listing — and unless every category fits under the 2999-page cap. A page past a category's end answers its props with no offers.
- **Drift is handled at the tail.** Offers removed mid-walk may empty a category's last page or two (`infojobsDriftPages`); an empty page with offers after it fails the crawl. Offers published mid-walk push the category's OLDEST offers past its stated end — the same offers every run, so a systematic miss the 48 h window would not absorb — so the walk keeps reading past the stated last page until one comes back empty.
- **`fullCatalog`.** The walk reads the whole catalogue or fails, so infojobs is swept by source. It used to be company-scoped, which can only close a company a run WROTE a posting for; with hydration failing (below) a run wrote ~6k refreshes, and on 2026-10-30 22,796 of its 28,835 open postings had gone unseen for over a week. Of four sampled, two redirected to `offer-no-accesible` (gone) and two still served the offer, published early September — old enough to sit in the tail the old walk could not reach.
- **Offer pages answer 405 after a few dozen per browser session.** 2026-10-30: the first ~45 detail pages of a run were served, then 942 of the next ~950 answered 405 until a session retired by age, which served ~13 more; the same pages answered 200 a few minutes later. 405 is how this bot wall refuses (ePreselec, the same owner's ATS, does it too), but the service retired a session only on 403/429, so the flagged exit served the rest of the session and a run hydrated ~60 new offers against a ~51k backlog. The service now retires a browser session on 405 as well (`_SESSION_REFUSED` in `services/scrapling-fetch/engines.py`), and the adapter sends a 405'd offer page once more (`infojobsDetailAttempts`), which opens on the next sticky exit. Listing pages are not affected (2,965 of 2,965 served).
