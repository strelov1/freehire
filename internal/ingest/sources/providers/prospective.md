**Prospective traps** (`prospective`; live-verified 2026-10-29):

- **JobCloud's Swiss ATS behind big employers' own career domains** (jobs.coopjobs.ch, jobs.admin.ch, jobs.stadlerrail.ch, jobs.usz.ch, jobs.fenaco.com), all a front end over one public JSON API on `ohws.prospective.ch`. The board is a **MEDIUM id**, one publication channel of a customer; there is no list of them, so they were found by sweeping ids 1,000,000–1,009,999 and 1–2,999 at one a second (175 media with jobs, 2026-10-29) — an unknown medium answers **400**, a known empty one `{"total":0}`.
- **One customer runs several media that list the same vacancies, and each medium republishes them under its OWN job id and viewkey** (Coop 1000103 and 1000192 share 95% of their URL slugs and no id; jobs.admin.ch runs six media, a German, French and Italian copy of the same ~390 vacancies, twice). Ids cannot dedupe them, so boards were chosen by URL slug: one medium per careers host, plus a second only when its slugs are mostly new (stadtluzern, baselland, ekz apprenticeships), and never a language twin. Two media of one vacancy set would be every posting stored twice.
- **The listing is read whole, to its natural end** (a short page; `fullBoardListing`), in pages of 200 on the long-timeout client — 1,000 of Coop's jobs took 66 s. **The stated `total` is not exact**: it differs from what the listing returns by one or two even on a single page (12 of 165 media on 2026-10-29), so it is logged, never enforced. `lang=` does not filter.
- **The body is in tenant-defined `sza_*` fields** (introduction, tasks, requirements, benefits, company profile …); the page's JobPosting `description` is sometimes only the intro and a contact (fenaco). **The employer is NOT in the listing** — a group's medium carries its subsidiaries (Volg, LANDI, TRAVECO under fenaco; SECO, fedpol under the federal administration) — so each new job is hydrated from `/public/v1/jobs/<viewkey>` for `hiringOrganization`, `datePosted` and `employmentType`. **Some customers' pages carry no JobPosting at all** (fenaco, Helvetia, Bühler: 11 of 137 media) — such a job keeps the board's employer and the listing's `start_date`, since the listing already holds its body; only a failed request is an unreadable marker. Titles may carry `<BR />`.
- **A job whose directlink is `/public/v1/redirect/…/external_url` is hosted elsewhere** (medium 1001986: 6,368 HGK jobs on hgk.de) and is skipped.
