**Dutch public boards** (`werkenvoornederland`, `gemeentebanen`, `academictransfer`; live-verified 2026-10-25). Each is one posting sitemap naming the open set, and each new posting is hydrated from its page's JobPosting at one request a second (ten seconds on AcademicTransfer). A government or university appointment starts as a fixed-term contract, so `employmentType: TEMPORARY` is NOT contract work there — `schemaScheduleType` maps only an internship and a single full- or part-time value.

- **`werkenvoornederland`** (central government, 1,364 postings, ~40 a day; vakgebied ICT on 15 of 60 sampled; "thuiswerken"/"hybride" in the body of 0 of 60). The identity is the code that ends the path (`CJIB-2026-9570`, `DEF2644-2005-6416`), not the title slug. The JobPosting `description` is a one-line summary, so the body is the page's sections ("Dit ga je doen", "Dit vragen wij", ...) — every `<section>` holding an `.s-article-content`, minus `stel_gerust_je_vraag`, the recruiter's contact card with phone numbers. The field (vakgebied, possibly several, comma-joined) is read from the page's `dataLayer.push`; ICT or "Development en testing" sets `IsTechHint`. `addressLocality` may carry the office street in brackets. A closed posting is a 404.
- **werkenbijdeoverheid.nl is NOT a second source**: its vacancy and internship sitemaps (1,584) hold every werkenvoornederland posting (1,364 of 1,364) plus ~220 from provinces, municipalities and water boards — but its pages carry no JobPosting, and a municipal posting names only the department ("Control") as the employer. Municipalities come through `gemeentebanen` instead.
- **`gemeentebanen`** (municipalities, 603 postings, ~40 a day; ~10% technical by title). `/vacatures/<id>/<employer>/<title>`, id is the identity; the sitemap also names `/vacatures` itself. The JobPosting carries the whole body. `employmentType` is FULL_TIME on 40 of 40 sampled, 32-hour ones included, so it is not read. the Dutch word for hybrid ("hybride") appears in ~70% of bodies, as a benefit line, and no structured field states a work mode. A closed posting 301s to `/vacatures/`; the redirect is the verdict.
- **`academictransfer`** (universities, UMCs, institutes; 767 postings — 497 academic, 291 support; ~20% technical by title). robots.txt sets `Crawl-delay: 10`, honoured for every request, so one run hydrates at most 240 (40 minutes) and the first catalogue takes four runs. The JobPosting is the `mainEntity` of a WebPage block, which `jobPostingNodes` unwraps. The search API (`api.academictransfer.com/vacancies/`) answers 401 and its robots.txt disallows everything. A posting is listed once, under `/en/` or `/nl/`; a closed one is a 404.
- **Not built, with numbers.** `werk.nl` (UWV, 269,655 postings): of 197 sampled details none was a UWV-native posting — every one came from a partner feed (`source` DGR 69%, then JIT, IGB, JOO, RUS, KNO) with an external apply URL, and every description is cut at ~2,000 characters; ~2% technical, ~6% mention remote or hybrid. That is the opposite of `arbeitsagentur`'s rule (only first-party postings), and the listing also needs an Oracle Access Manager cookie dance plus an XSRF header, pages 20 at a time behind a 10,000-page cap, and `www.werk.nl` serves the wrong intermediate certificate (DigiCert Global G2 instead of the "G2 TLS EU RSA4096 SHA384 2022 CA1" its leaf names), which Go on the crawl host rejects. Nationale Vacaturebank (~90k): every page answers DPG Media's consent gate, and werk.nl itself lists it as an apply target, i.e. it is an aggregator.
