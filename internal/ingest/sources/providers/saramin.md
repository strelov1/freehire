**saramin traps** (`saramin`; measured 2026-11-22 from the prod crawl host, plain HTTP, no proxy):

- **Boardless aggregator, the whole catalogue, every trade.** The adapter reads Saramin's own category listing, `/zf_user/jobs/list/job-category?cat_mcls=<n>&page=<p>&page_count=100`, for the 21 top-level categories (`cat_mcls` 2..22; 1, 23 and 24 answer an empty page). Category totals (the `total_count` figure on page 1) sum to ~290k memberships; a posting may sit in several categories, so the distinct count is lower and unmeasured. The pager runs to its natural end (cat 2: 10,503 postings, page 106 holds the last three) with no depth cap. `/zf_user/jobs/list/domestic` and a category-less query answered 200 with no cards, so the category parameter is required.
- **robots.txt (`User-agent: *`) is long and does not touch what is read.** Disallowed: `/feed.php`, `/ad*`, `/zf_user/auth`, `/zf_user/mandb`, `/zf_user/talent` (except `/browse-resume`), `/zf_user/jobs/view/etc`, `/zf_user/recruit/view/`, `/zf_user/special-recruit/`, `/zf_user/company/`, `/recruit/recruit_view.php`, `/zf_user/member/*`, `/zf_user/persons/` and more. Not disallowed, hence read: `/zf_user/jobs/list/job-category` and `/zf_user/jobs/relay/view-detail`. `GPTBot` and `Bytespider` are banned outright; the adapter's User-Agent is the browser-compatible form of the project's own (`compatibleUASource`). The sitemaps (`/sitemap.xml`, `/sitemapindex.xml`) list only landing pages (`/zf_user/jobs/list/...`), no posting, so they are no route.
- **A card is enough for a stored posting; a new one costs one request.** The listing row carries `rec_idx`, title, employer, a place ("서울 강남구 외", the first of several), `경력 1년↑ · 정규직` (experience and employment type), education, the deadline (`~12.19(토)`, `D-3`, `상시채용`), `5일 전 등록` and up to five occupation tags. The body is not in the page: the posting page (`/zf_user/jobs/relay/view?rec_idx=`) loads it by an iframe, `/zf_user/jobs/relay/view-detail?rec_idx=<id>&rec_seq=0`, which answers a plain GET with `<div class="user_content">` (9-40 KB in the two sampled). A stored posting is yielded as `SeenRefresh`.
- **Never fetch `/zf_user/jobs/relay/view` without a Referer and `Accept-Language`.** From the crawl host it answers 200 with a DECOY: the same unrelated Italian "Autogrill" JobPosting (258 KB, ld+json) for every `rec_idx`, which would store one wrong posting under every id. With a Referer and `Accept-Language: ko-KR` it returns the real page, but that page has no body and no ld+json, which is why the iframe route is used instead. `view-detail` answered the same body with or without them (checked on two ids) and carries no ld+json either. The adapter requires `div.user_content` and otherwise defers the posting.
- **Some bodies are pictures.** Employers publish their posting as image banners (`user_content` holding `<img>` only; one of the two sampled bodies is mostly images with a text paragraph, and the share of image-only ones is unmeasured). Such a posting is stored with a body built from the card's own facts (tags, experience, education, place) so it is not asked for again every run.
- **Rate limit: 300 requests per 60 s** (`X-RateLimit-Limit`, `-Reset`) on every route. Pages cost ~270 KB gzipped (2.6 MB raw, 2.6 s) however few cards they carry, so `page_count=100` is cheapest. The adapter paces at 4 a second across 5 category workers; the whole walk is ~2,900 pages, ~25 minutes, ~800 MB on the wire, plus up to 3,000 body reads (`saraminMaxNewPerRun`, newest id first) so the cold catalogue drains over several runs. Any listing page failing fails the crawl (a short list would mass-close by company); a body failing is deferred. Suggested cadence 12 h, `INGEST_RUN_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` 3600. Estimates, not a measured full run.
- **The sweep grace is 72 h.** The walk is the only evidence of what is live. Postings can move between pages while the walk runs, so one may be missed for a run; the next lists it again. Not `fullBoardListing` and not `fullCatalog`.
- **`IsTechHint` comes from the source's own category.** A posting listed under `cat_mcls=2` ("IT개발·데이터") carries it; others are left to the dictionaries. The flag is merged after all categories are read, so it does not depend on walk order.
- **Overlap with `jumpit` and `recruiter`.** `jumpit` is Saramin's IT-only board and its external ids are the same `rec_idx` numbers (55441892, ...), so IT postings appear under both keys; the cross-source duplicate suppression is what collapses them. `recruiter` is recruiter.co.kr, Saramin's ATS, a different site and ids.
- **Korean titles resolve almost no category.** Measured on 100 titles each of categories 2, 11 and 8: `classify.Parse` gave a category for 3 of 100 IT-category titles and 0 of 100 in each other; seniority 0 of 300; `IsTech` 1 of 100 in the IT category. Most titles are an employer's generic announcement ("각 부문 신입 및 경력 채용공고"). Expect ~97% category-unresolved, so search (which withholds a posting with no category) shows almost nothing without a Korean classify pass; the card's occupation tags (`job_sector`, e.g. "백엔드/서버개발") are not mapped and would be the better signal. `IsTechHint` keeps the IT-category postings technical meanwhile.
- **The official Open API** (`oapi.saramin.co.kr`) needs an access key and was not used.
- **Unverified.** The distinct posting count, the year of a `~MM.DD` deadline across new year (read as next year when more than 90 days past), and whether the default listing order drops a posting between pages are not measured.
