**Unstop traps** (`unstop`; live-verified 2026-11-22 from the crawl host, plain HTTP, no proxy):

- **robots.txt allows the route.** `User-agent: *` allows `/job/`, `/internship/` and `/api/public/*` and disallows the rest of `/api/*`, `/u/`, `/p/`, `/directory/user/*`. `Crawl-delay: 1`, honoured by `pacedUnstopGetter`. The adapter reads only `/api/public/opportunity/search-result`, the endpoint the site's own listing pages call. It answers 200 to a plain client, with or without a browser User-Agent.
- **Jobs and internships are two feeds of one endpoint** (`opportunity=jobs`, `opportunity=internships`; an internship row carries `subtype: "internships"`). Competitions, hackathons, quizzes and courses are other `opportunity` values and are not postings; they are never requested.
- **`oppstatus=open` is load-bearing.** Without it each feed reports `total: 10000` and `last_page: 667` at 15 per page, and page 668 is a 500: it is a 10,000-row window of OPEN AND EXPIRED postings, newest first, not the catalogue. With `oppstatus=open` the total is the real open set: 969 jobs and 661 internships on 2026-11-22, 10 and 7 pages at `per_page=100`. `per_page=100` is honoured (`per_page` above that was not tried). A page past `last_page` answers 200 with an empty list.
- **Everything is inline, no detail request.** Each row carries the full HTML body (`details`), `organisation.name`, `locations[]` (`city`, `state`, `country`), `approved_date` (`2026-11-21 23:15:14 GMT+0530`), `end_date` (the application deadline) and `jobDetail` (`type` wfh / in_office / hybrid / on_field, `timing` full_time / part_time / contractual, salary bounds, `currency`, `pay_in`, `show_salary`). The id is the numeric `id`, stable; `seo_url` is the page.
- **Work mode.** `wfh` is remote, `in_office` onsite, `hybrid` hybrid. `on_field` (sales visits; 14 of 100 sampled jobs) states no mode on purpose.
- **Salary is a number in rupees, `fa-rupee` being a font-icon class, not a currency code.** `pay_in` is `annually` or `monthly`; internships are all monthly. It is read only when `show_salary = 1`, `not_disclosed` is false and a bound is above zero. Jobs state a CTC in rupees (₹3,00,000 reads 300000); no lakh parsing is needed because the field is numeric.
- **Internships take `EmploymentType = internship`** whatever their `timing`. They are real postings with a stipend, mostly work from home.
- **Geography.** Every location measured named India (123 of 123 jobs rows, 85 of 85 internship rows); about half the internships and 6% of jobs carry no location at all (work from home). The adapter sets only `FallbackCountries = in`, the weak signal, and puts the places in `Location` as `City, State, Country` joined with `; ` (a posting can name up to 14 cities). `locations[]` states places, not that the work is there, so no `Countries`.
- **Not IT-first, no `IsTechHint`.** The catalogue is mostly sales, business development, operations and marketing; the `workfunction` and `required_skills` fields are largely AI-generated (`pivot.ai_generated`) and are not read. Of a 100-job sample about 2 carried a software workfunction, and the internships have a few web and design roles. Expect a low IT share (single-digit percent) and, being English, a title classify gap only on the Indian-specific titles ("BDE", "Telecaller", "Campus Ambassador").
- **Complete-or-fail.** The feed is offset-paged over a live set, so a posting expiring mid-crawl can shift a row past a page boundary. `FullCatalog` is granted because the crawl fails on any page error, an empty feed, or more than `unstopCountTolerance` (2%) of the reported total missing, never because the count matched exactly.
- **Budget.** About 19 requests a run (10 + 7 pages plus the first), ~20 s at the 1 s pacing. Cadence: every 6 hours is plenty for ~1,600 postings; a unit timeout of 10 minutes is ample.
- **Not read: `min_experience`/`max_experience`** (null on every measured row, so its type is unknown), `required_skills`, `filters` (eligibility such as Fresher), registration and view counts.
