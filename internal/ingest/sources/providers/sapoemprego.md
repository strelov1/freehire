**SAPO Emprego traps** (`sapoemprego`; live-verified 2026-10-27 from a residential IP and the crawl host):

- **Plain HTTP passes; `robots.txt` names no Claude agent** and disallows `/offers/search`, `/company/`, `/apply-job/` and the per-offer report/contact/email actions — not `/offers/<slug>?id=` or the sitemaps.
- **The listing is the offers sitemap, `/offers.xml`: ~23k entries (~5 MB) on 2026-10-27, the whole live catalogue** (the listing page states 23,444 offers). It carries only each posting's URL and `lastmod`. The id is the URL's `id` parameter (a UUID); the path slug is cosmetic. `lastmod` moves when an agency bumps a posting (~3.6k a day), so "most recently touched first" is the order, not posting age.
- **There is no cheaper listing.** The `/offers` and `/offers/company/<slug>/offers` pages server-render only their first 9-11 cards (`:offers` props, which DO name the employer and a `remote_work` flag); every further page comes from `/offers/search`, robots-disallowed and reCAPTCHA-validated. `?pagina=2` answers an empty list.
- **So the employer is unknown until the page is bought, and the adapter is NOT `CoverageGated`.** ~1,300 postings (~6%) come from employers already covered by a non-aggregator source (GI Group alone posts 848, Manpower 197, measured against prod 2026-10-27) and are discarded unsaved; ~2.5% of a 275-card listing sample hid the employer. Both stay unseen and are re-bought, so the run picks its pages with `hydrationLanes` (xing's fresh + moving backlog window): 800 freshest + 400 from a window that moves every hour.
- **The posting page's schema.org JobPosting is the body**: `title`, `description` (HTML), `datePosted` (a bare date), `hiringOrganization.name` (+ `url`), `jobLocation` as ONE Place (`addressLocality`/`addressRegion`, `addressCountry` "PT"; no locality for "Todo o país"), `employmentType` FULL_TIME/PART_TIME, `validThrough`, `industry` (the first of up to two categories) and `jobLocationType: TELECOMMUTE` only on fully remote postings. A hidden employer is `hiringOrganization.name: "Anónima"` and is dropped, as catho drops "Empresa Confidencial". An id the site no longer holds answers **200 with the search page** (no JobPosting), read as gone.
- **The "Resumo da Oferta" list (`div.metadata`) states what the JSON-LD does not**: `li.workhome` is Presencial / Híbrido / Teletrabalho on every posting (the only hybrid signal), `li.salary` an annual band ("De 15.000€ a 25.000€"; "A definir" on ~93%, read only in the De-X-a-Y form).
- **Shares, 2026-10-27**: "Informática e Tecnologias" 1,441 of 23,444 (6.1%, sets `IsTechHint`); Teletrabalho 150 (0.6%) and Híbrido 1,160 (4.9%) site-wide, 81 and 658 of the IT category; roughly 40-45% of postings come from staffing agencies (Adecco, Eurofirms, GI Group, Randstad, Job Impulse, D'accord, ...), filed under the agency's name.
- **The run is bounded, and stops short of its deadline.** One limiter paces every request at one a second with three workers (a page answers in ~0.6 s from the crawl host, ~2 s from a laptop); a run reads at most 1,200 pages and stops hydrating `sapoempregoDeadlineMargin` (5 min) before its context deadline — a hydrating crawl writes nothing until `FetchNew` returns. The first ~19 runs drain the catalogue.
