**Phenom traps** (`phenom`; live-verified 2026-10-30 from a residential IP and the crawl host, over all 383 catalogued boards):

- **One pace serves every board, so the run's request count is what decides whether it finishes.** All boards share `pacedPhenomGetter`, and until 2026-10-30 every crawl re-read every stored posting's `jobDetail` at 1 req/s. When the catalogue went from ~95 boards to 383 (2026-10-21..30), a run reached ~18 boards before its deadline and 66% of the ~122k open postings went unseen for over a week. It is now a `HydratingSource`: a stored posting is a `SeenRefresh`, and new ones are hydrated within `phenomMaxNewPerBoard` and the run-wide `phenomMaxNewPerRun`. A full listing at the API's 500-row page is ~1,250 requests at 3 req/s. The boards stated ~514k postings that day, so the first weeks of runs drain a ~390k hydration backlog.
- **`size` is capped at 500 and `from+size` at 10,000.** Asked for 1,000 rows, the API answers 500. A page reaching past the 10,000th result answers empty with `totalHits: 0`. A board stating more (2026-10-31: CVS and O'Reilly ~19.1k, Advance Auto Parts 16.5k, Dollar Tree 15.4k) is therefore read **one US state at a time** through the `selected_fields` facet filter (`{"state": ["Ohio"]}`). The board's first page asks for the per-state counts (`counts` + `all_fields: ["state"]`), so a board under the window pays no extra request. The crawl fails the board unless every state holds at most 9,500 postings (so the last page's `from+size` stays inside the window however the pages fell), the counts add up to at least the stated total, and every counted state's own slice states a total between 1 and 9,500 (an empty or oversized slice fails even when its initial count passed). The sum may exceed it, because a multi-location posting is counted and listed under each of its states (CVS: 23,224 for 19,099). Category and country cannot slice these boards: Advance Auto Parts and Dollar Tree hold ~99% of their postings under one category, and all four are ~all US.
- **A slice that fits one page is read whole; offset paging loses postings only between pages.** Walking CVS by state on 2026-10-31, every state with ≤500 postings returned exactly its count, while California read 1,154 of 1,508 and Pennsylvania 1,097 of 1,453. The state walk read 94.6% of CVS's distinct postings, the category walk (fewer, larger slices) 86.4%. That is the lever if a board's per-walk coverage ever has to be raised further: slices of ≤500.
- **Offset paging is not stable, so the adapter does NOT claim `fullBoardListing`.** Two walks of `careers.kohls.com` a minute apart read 4,403 and 4,398 distinct postings of a stated 5,211, and together exactly 5,211. `careers.dhl.com` read 92% of its postings at 500 a page and 83% at 100. `sortBy: "Most recent"` does not fix it. One walk therefore proves nothing about the postings it missed. Successive walks miss different ones, which is what keeps the company-scoped 48h sweep sound, but it closes only on a run that finishes.
