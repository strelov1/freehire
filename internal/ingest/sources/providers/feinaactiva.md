**Feina Activa traps** (`feinaactiva`; live-verified 2026-10-27 from a residential IP and the crawl host):

- **The listing is the SPA's own JSON, `GET /api/offers/list?page=N`, and it carries each offer in full** (title, employer, place, summary, duties, requirements, conditions) — no posting page is bought. There is no `robots.txt` (the SPA answers its HTML shell for it). `total` was 5,694 on 2026-10-27.
- **Ten offers a page, whatever you ask**: `size`/`pageSize`/`per_page` are ignored and `limit`/`offset` turn the answer into an error, so a walk is ~570 requests at ~1.5 s each. The adapter therefore implements `StreamingSource` and emits each page as it arrives — a run cut short keeps what it read.
- **Without a sort the order reshuffles between requests**: a full walk in the default order read only 4,296 distinct offers of 5,694. `sort=publishedAt&order=desc` is stable (two walks identical). **Pages are 1-based; `page=0` repeats page 1.**
- **About a quarter of offers hide the employer, in three shapes, and are dropped** (as catho drops "Empresa Confidencial"): `business.hasName=false`; a DESCRIPTION in place of the name with `hasName=true` ("Empresa de serveis de neteja", "Empresa del sector de les TIC", "Empresa pública" — a real name opening with "Empresa" carries its legal form); and "Oficina de Treball del SOC", a SOC office posting on an employer's behalf. Measured over 4,493 offers: 2,179 named, 1,326 CIDO, 383 hasName=false, 299 descriptions, 306 SOC office.
- **`origin: "diba"` offers (~30%) are public-sector calls relayed from the Diputació de Barcelona's CIDO bulletin**: the employer is the council or body, the title ends in " CIDO" (stripped), the duties read "Veure convocatòria" (dropped from the body), `externalUrl` points at cido.diba.cat, and some are standing pools open since 2021–22.
- **The place is `<PROVINCE> - <COMARCA> - <postcode> <MUNICIPALITY>` in capitals**, with the article moved to the end ("17100 BISBAL D'EMPORDÀ, LA") and Barcelona's postcode missing its leading zero ("8029"); it is rendered "La Bisbal d'Empordà, Girona" and sets `Countries` es. Salary is a condition line, "Salari mensual brut des de '1800' fins a '2500'" or "Salari mensual brut 1695.43" (EUR a month). "Jornada completa/parcial" is the employment type; "Contracte indefinit/temporal" is the contract's term and sets nothing. No structured category or remote field exists (~2% mention teletreball in text), so neither `IsTechHint` nor `WorkMode` is set; ~3% of titles are technical.
- **Offer URL is `/search/offers/detail/<reference>`** (FA9232xxxx employer offers, 09202xxxxxx SOC-office offers, FA2026MMDDOnn CIDO calls); the reference is the external id.
