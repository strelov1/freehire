**Emply traps** (`emply`; live-verified 2026-10-19 from a residential IP and fsn):

- **One keyless listing is the whole crawl.** `POST https://<host>/api/integration/vacancy/get-page` with `{"count","offset","filters":[]}` answers `{"vacancies":[...],"count":<total>}`, and each vacancy carries its full HTML body in every language it is written in — no detail request. **`filters` is required**: without it the endpoint answers 500. The career site's own script also sends a `sectionId`; it is not needed, and omitting it only adds the talent-pool entry, which the adapter drops anyway.
- **The board is a slug OR a host.** Most sites are `<slug>.career.emply.com`, but a large share run on the customer's own domain (`midtjob.dk` — Region Midtjylland, the biggest at 305 — `job.frederiksberg.dk`, `jobs.bdo.dk`) with the same endpoint. `emplyHost` tells them apart by the dot and `BoardDedupeKey` folds `<slug>` and `<slug>.career.emply.com` into one target. Only the `career.emply.com` form is recognised by `atsboard`; a customer domain is a vanity host. `*.emply.com` without `career.` and `*.emply.net` are other Emply products (an HR suite, an ADFS login), not career sites.
- **A gone tenant redirects to `https://emply.com/`** (302, also for the POST), so it surfaces as a JSON decode error on the marketing page, not as a status. Old tenants are often renamed with a `-v2`/`-1` suffix rather than reused; the bare name then 500s or redirects.
- **`talentPool: true` is the standing "unsolicited application" entry**, not a vacancy. Some tenants publish an open application as an ordinary vacancy instead; nothing structured tells it apart.
- **`languageKey` is the tenant's language setting, not the text's.** `init` labels Danish postings `en-GB`. The adapter prefers an `en-*` translation and otherwise the first (primary) one, and the URL's trailing `/<lang>` opens the page in the language read. The ad page is keyed by `shortId` alone — any slug resolves.
- **Facts are tenant-defined.** Employment type, workplace, contact and so on live in `factDatas` under titles each tenant names itself (`Beskæftigelsestype`, `Stillingstype`, ` Position type`), so none is mapped.
- **`fullBoardListing`**: the walk pages by offset to `count` and fails unless the distinct ids equal it. The endpoint honours large page sizes (305 in one response), so paging is a safeguard, not a limit.
