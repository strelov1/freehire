**Optius traps** (`optius`, a Slovenian general job board at <https://www.optius.com>, boardless; live-verified 2026-10-29 from a residential IP):

- **The listing is server-rendered HTML, `/iskalci/prosta-delovna-mesta/?s=<offset>`, twenty cards a page, newest first**, and its `div.result-count` states the total (1,310 on 2026-10-29, 66 pages). The walk reads until the first cardless page at an offset past the total that page states (the cardless page's own count, so postings expiring mid-walk do not fail it); a page without the result counter, or a cardless one short of its total, is no answer and fails the crawl, as does any listing page failing, since a partial walk would let the company-scoped sweep close postings it never reached. The job sitemap (`/sitemap.xml/sitemap/Job/{1,2}`) names exactly the same 1,310 postings but carries no employer, which the sweep and the coverage gate need, so it is not used. `robots.txt` disallows only `/admin`.
- **It answers 429 to as few as two concurrent requests** (nginx), and to one at a time paced at one a second (the twelfth listing page), while requests back to back at the ~4 s a page takes to render never drew one. So one request is in flight at a time, at most one every three seconds: a live walk of both listings (72 pages, 1,310 postings) and six posting pages took 3m56s with no refusal. Hence at most 400 posting pages a run, newest first, stopping 5 minutes before the deadline; a cold catalogue is taken over four runs. The project's own User-Agent is served.
- **A card carries the title, the employer (`.company-name`), the place ("Kraj dela") and the application deadline — never the body.** The `Prijave do` date is read from the card on every crawl, including seen-only refreshes: the pipeline persists it without rewriting hydrated content, so a shortened or extended deadline reaches stored postings. A missing or invalid date preserves the stored one. Every card names its employer. The posting id is the number the path ends with, written twice (`...-mz-965281-965281/`).
- **The body is the posting page's main column, `section.job-detail-col`**: the employer's introduction under its name, then section headings ("Opis delovnega mesta", "Pričakujemo", "Ponujamo") and labelled fact rows (`div.info-cols`). Employers with their own page template (Krka) keep the same headings and rows. A posting that is only an image (Telekom) or an embedded third-party page (`iframe.job-iframe`, the agency Kariera) has no text there, and its body is the JobPosting's `description`, `responsibilities` and `jobBenefits`. Every sampled page (33 of 33) carries a JobPosting, whose strings hold raw newlines (`ldJobPosting` repairs them). It adds `datePosted`, `validThrough` (the source's own last application day, read into `ApplicationDeadline`), `workHours` (`polni`/`skrajšani delovni čas` read as full/part time) and the experience band ("1 - 3 let", lower bound only; "0 - 1 leto" states no minimum). Its `employmentType` is the contract's term (fixed or open-ended), which the vocabulary does not carry, and its `industry` lists several areas at once — a Telekom shop assistant's includes "Informatika, Programiranje" — so it is no tech signal. Salary is free text ("Mesečno plačilo po avstrijski kolektivni pogodbi"), not read.
- **Geography: Slovenia is the FALLBACK country, never the stated one, and not for postings abroad.** The site's region filter has a "Tujina" (abroad) slice, `/iskalci/prosta-delovna-mesta/regija/tujina/` (89 postings, almost all "Avstrija, <town>"); it is walked first and its postings get no fallback, so "2482 Münchendorf" is not filed under Slovenia. About half the places are Slovenian towns and regions the location dictionary does not know (Kamnik, Domžale, Osrednjeslovenska), which the fallback files under Slovenia. A region place carries its vehicle-registration code ("Podravska - MB , Slovenija"), which the dictionary read as Manitoba and Korea; the adapter drops it.
- **A gone posting** answers 404. A posting path the site does not recognise is redirected to a filtered listing, so a page answered with a listing in its place is read as gone too; any other page carrying no posting is no answer, and the posting waits for the next run.
