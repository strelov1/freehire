**ITviec traps** (`itviec`; live-verified 2026-10-15):

- **Reachable only from the crawl host.** From a residential IP and from a US datacenter, every path — `robots.txt` included — answers 403 with `cf-mitigated: challenge`, an INTERACTIVE Turnstile that neither `fingerprintHTTP` nor a headless Chrome clears. From the prod crawl host, plain HTTP answers 200 on the listing (`Mozilla/5.0` and `Go-http-client/2.0` UAs, ten sequential requests, no refusal). Validate from the crawl host; a local 403 says nothing about prod. `robots.txt` disallows only `/subscriptions/new`.
- **Boardless: one listing is the whole catalogue.** `/it-jobs?page=N` serves ~20 cards a page; the header said "658 IT jobs" and pages 1-33 held exactly 658 cards (32×20+18), page 34 none. Not `fullBoardListing`: that match is one snapshot, not a proven bar.
- **Read CARDS, not posting links.** Every page, including the one past the end, also links two "company spotlight" postings (`?lab_feature=company_spotlight`) that are not cards. A link-based walk never sees the end and reads the decoys as postings. The card element carries `data-search--job-selection-job-slug-value` and `data-job-key`.
- **The external id is `data-job-key`, not the slug's trailing number.** The suffix is four digits (`-0103`) — 10,000 values, so a catalogue that accumulates thousands of stored postings collides by construction. The key is the posting's own (the posting page's save-job element carries the same value as its id); 57 of 58 sampled are UUIDs and one is `e9661b73-ffd`, so it is treated as opaque.
- **The body is the ld+json `description`, and it is not HTML.** It is newline-separated lines: the four fixed section headings ("Top 3 Reasons To Join Us", "The Job", "Your Skills and Experience", "Why You'll Love Working Here") and the "top 3 reasons" as bare text, with each employer section as HTML. `jobBenefits` repeats the last section and is not read.
- **The ld+json `title` strips punctuation** ("AI Software Engineer Python/Go/C/C++ AI Agents"); the page's `<h1>` keeps it, so the title is read from there.
- **Salary is structured but often withheld.** The page hides pay behind sign-in, yet the ld+json `baseSalary` carries `minValue`/`maxValue`/`unitText` when the employer published a range (`800`-`2000` USD/MONTH). A withheld one carries the phrase "You'll love it" and no bound, and yields nothing.
- **`addressLocality` is the literal "Not Available"**; the city is `addressRegion` ("Hà Nội", "Hồ Chí Minh"). The work arrangement is the header badge (`At office`/`Hybrid`/`Remote`), not ld+json. `employmentType` was `FULL_TIME` on both sampled pages and `monthsOfExperience` read 10 and 37, so neither is mapped until a wider sample shows they mean something.
- **"ITviec Recruitment Consulting" is ITviec headhunting for an unnamed client** (4 of 58 sampled cards). It is stored under that name; the real employer is not stated anywhere on the page.
- **Paced at 1 req/s (`itviecRequestInterval`), unmeasured.** A third party saw 429 on back-to-back sequential listing sweeps (career-ops, 2026-08-23); nobody has measured the budget from prod. A cold run is ~690 requests (~12 min); steady state is the 34 listing pages plus new postings. The first 403/429 defers the rest of the run's hydration.
