**CBOP traps** (`cbop`; live-verified 2026-10-29 from the crawl host):

- **Poland's public job board** (Centralna Baza Ofert Pracy, oferty.praca.gov.pl): 18,290 offers on 2026-10-29 (50,279 vacancies), ~650 added a working day, 98% registered by district labour offices (PUP), the rest by voivodeship offices and the youth labour corps. Every trade, mostly manual and public-sector work; ~0.7% technical by title (school IT teachers, municipal IT staff). ~170 are abroad (Germany, Norway, Austria), named by their Polish country name. No robots.txt (404).
- **The portal's HTML is behind an F5 JavaScript challenge** (TSPD); only a browser passes it. **Its search API is not**: `POST /portal-api/v3/oferta/wyszukiwanie?page=N&size=100` with body `{}` answers a plain client from the crawl host, newest first, `totalElements` stating the size. Paths found in the portal's JS bundle.
- **A row the service cannot render fails its whole page**: HTTP 200 with `"status": 500` and a null payload. Page 27 at size 100 failed every time. The walk re-reads a refused page as ten pages of ten, and a refused one of those as single rows, so a bad row costs only itself (5 of 18,290 on 2026-10-29). A whole walk took 8.7 minutes from the crawl host (~1.8 s a page, one request a second) and kept 16,882 offers. Losing more than 1% of the catalogue, or any transport failure, fails the crawl.
- **The row is the whole offer**: duties (`zakresObowiazkow`) and requirements (`wymagania`) as plain text, contract (`rodzajUmowy`), post share (`wymiarZatrud`: `1`, `0.5`, `3/4`, `nie dotyczy`), pay. No detail request is made. The offer page is `/portal/lista-ofert/szczegoly-oferty/<id>`.
- **7.7% withhold the employer** ("kontakt przez PUP": apply through the labour office), including every offer reserved for registered jobseekers. Those are dropped.
- **Pay is a sentence, not a field** ("od 4 806 PLN", "od 5 178 do 6 397 PLN Wysokość wynagrodzenia … Karta Nauczyciela", an intern's "5 000 PLN /godz."). The portal files it as gross, but the period is unstated unless "/godz." appears. It goes into the description, never into the structured salary. No structured remote field exists.
