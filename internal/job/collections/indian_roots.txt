# indian-roots collection membership (canonical company slugs, one per line).
# Companies founded in India or by Indian founders, operating internationally —
# the Indian counterpart of eastern_roots.txt. Three strands sit in one list because
# "Indian roots" is one fact about the company: the IT-services majors, the domestic
# product/startup ecosystem (incl. the unicorns), and the Indian-founder diaspora
# companies headquartered abroad (Zscaler, Nutanix, Rubrik, Cohesity, Glean, ...).
#
# Sources: the Indian unicorn lists published by Failory and FounderPin (both
# tracking the same Tracxn/Inc42 ecosystem count, ~130 unicorns as of Aug 2026),
# Wikipedia's list of Indian IT companies, a hand seed for the diaspora strand, and
# — the largest source by far — our own catalogue: the companies already carrying
# hq_country = India, plus those whose enrichment tagline names India.
#
# Mining the catalogue needs one guard the external lists do not: `Indiana`. A
# tagline predicate on "indian" matches the US state and returned Butler University,
# the Archdiocese of Indianapolis and a dozen Indiana hospitals. Match on a word
# boundary (Postgres `~* '\yindia\y'`), and exclude "American Indian" separately.
#
# hq_country is a hint, not a verdict — `accenture` carries India there (7,090 open
# jobs) and is an Irish-American firm with a large Indian arm. Every entry mined from
# it was read against its tagline before being written down. Excluded on that pass:
# companies that are not Indian, pure staffing and local contracting shops with no
# product of their own, universities and hospitals, and slugs too generic to be sure
# of (`flow`, `core`, `able`, `raven`, `streak` — the last collides with a US CRM).
#
# Every entry was verified present in the catalogue at curation time. The list
# deliberately does NOT carry marquee companies we do not ingest yet: an entry that
# matches nothing is indistinguishable from a typo in the import's unmatched report.
# Add a company when its board lands.
#
# Matched via normalize.CompanySlug at import (cmd/import-collections); unmatched
# slugs are logged by name. Lines starting with # are comments.
#
# Ambiguous single-word slugs are deliberately absent: `porter`, `locus` and `slice`
# in our catalogue are the US companies of those names, not the Indian ones.
